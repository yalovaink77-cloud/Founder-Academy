# Advertising Checklist

Advertising is ready when:

* [ ] Product-Market Fit is demonstrated.
* [ ] Positioning is clear.
* [ ] Pricing is validated.
* [ ] Landing page is complete.
* [ ] Analytics are configured.
* [ ] Conversion tracking is working.
* [ ] Budget is defined.
* [ ] Success metrics are documented.
* [ ] A testing plan is prepared.

Advertising should scale a proven system, not compensate for an unproven product.
