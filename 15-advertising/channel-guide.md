# Advertising Channels

Choose advertising channels based on customer behavior.

Common channels include:

* Meta Ads
* Google Ads
* Apple Search Ads
* TikTok Ads
* LinkedIn Ads
* Reddit Ads
* X Ads
* YouTube Ads

For every channel document:

* Target audience
* Cost
* Strengths
* Weaknesses
* Best use cases
* Success metrics

No advertising channel is universally the best. The right choice depends on the product and customer.
