# Principles

1. Think in systems, not individual products.

2. Learn principles before tools.

3. Validate before building.

4. Build before scaling.

5. Measure before optimizing.

6. Growth without retention is failure.

7. Every lesson must produce a reusable output.

8. Every framework must work across different products.

9. Case studies are examples, not rules.

10. Founder Academy is a living operating system.
