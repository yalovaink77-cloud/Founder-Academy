# Revenue Models

## Purpose

Define how the company creates sustainable income from customer value.

## Models

- Subscription
- Transaction
- Usage-based
- Freemium
- Marketplace commission
- Advertising

## Evaluate

Consider:

- Customer value.
- Predictability.
- Scalability.
- Retention.
- Complexity.

## Metrics

Track:

- Revenue.
- MRR.
- ARR.
- ARPU.
- LTV.
- Churn.

## Founder Academy Standard

Revenue should grow when customers receive increasing value.
