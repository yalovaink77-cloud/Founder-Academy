# Unit Economics

## Purpose

Measure whether each customer creates sustainable business value.

## Core Metrics

### CAC

Cost to acquire one customer.

### LTV

Value created by one customer over time.

### LTV:CAC

Relationship between customer value and acquisition cost.

### Payback Period

Time required to recover CAC.

### Gross Margin

Revenue remaining after direct costs.

## Questions

- Does each customer create value?
- Is acquisition sustainable?
- Which segments are profitable?
- Which channels work?

## Founder Academy Standard

Do not scale customer acquisition before understanding unit economics.
