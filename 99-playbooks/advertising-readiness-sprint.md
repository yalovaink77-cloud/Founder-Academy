# Advertising Readiness Sprint

## Purpose

Decide whether paid acquisition is ready to amplify a working product and business system, and define a bounded first advertising experiment when appropriate.

## Sprint Question

> If we buy more attention now, can we measure what happens and can the business learn from the spend?

## Principle

Advertising accelerates an existing system.

It does not create:

- Product-market fit.
- Clear positioning.
- Product value.
- Retention.
- Sustainable economics.

Do not use paid traffic to hide missing fundamentals.

## Readiness Gate

Evaluate the following before spending.

### 1. Customer

- Target customer/problem segment is defined.
- There is evidence the problem matters.

### 2. Offer and Positioning

- Core promise is clear.
- Message matches the audience.
- Offer/pricing is understandable.
- Important proof/trust signals exist.

### 3. Product

- Users can complete the critical journey.
- First value is observable.
- Critical blockers are controlled.

### 4. Measurement

- Traffic/source can be attributed well enough for the experiment.
- Conversion event is defined.
- Activation/quality after conversion can be observed.
- Revenue is measurable when relevant.

### 5. Retention and Value

- There is at least an early understanding of what happens after acquisition.
- Serious retention failure is not being ignored.

### 6. Economics

The founder understands, at an appropriate maturity level:

- What can be spent safely.
- What conversion/customer value would justify continued spend.
- Variable costs or margin where relevant.
- Why the test can create useful evidence even if it loses money within the approved learning budget.

## Readiness Decision

Choose one:

### READY FOR BOUNDED TEST

Fundamentals and measurement are sufficient to learn safely.

### NOT READY — FIX FOUNDATION

List the exact missing prerequisite and return to the appropriate earlier stage.

Do not launch paid campaigns merely to feel progress.

## First Paid Experiment

When ready, define:

### Hypothesis

What do we believe paid acquisition can prove?

### Audience

Who will receive the message?

### Offer / Message

What promise and action are being tested?

### Channel

Select based on customer behavior and test requirements, not trend popularity.

Platform-specific setup belongs to execution research because interfaces and policies change.

### Conversion Event

What action counts as meaningful conversion?

### Quality Event

What downstream signal distinguishes a valuable acquisition from a shallow click/signup?

### Budget Boundary

Define a founder-approved maximum learning budget.

### Stop Rule

State when the test stops because:

- Tracking is broken.
- Product experience is broken.
- Spend exceeds approved boundary.
- Results are clearly outside viable thresholds.
- Evidence is sufficient for a decision.

### Review Date / Sample Rule

Define when evidence will be reviewed without reacting emotionally to every short-term fluctuation.

## Analyze the Full Path

Do not evaluate only clicks or platform-reported conversion.

Where possible follow:

```text
Spend
↓
Qualified Reach
↓
Response / Click
↓
Conversion
↓
Activation / First Value
↓
Retention / Revenue Signal
```

## Founder Decision

Choose:

- Continue and refine.
- Test another message/audience/channel.
- Fix landing/product conversion.
- Fix activation/retention.
- Pause paid acquisition.
- Scale cautiously when evidence and economics justify it.

## Required Outputs

- Readiness assessment.
- Missing prerequisites, if any.
- Advertising hypothesis.
- Audience/message/channel definition.
- Measurement plan.
- Budget boundary.
- Stop rule.
- Result analysis.
- Founder decision.

## Exit Criteria

The founder understands whether advertising should happen now and why.

If advertising runs, the experiment has a bounded cost, measurable downstream quality, and a decision rule.

## AI Role

AI may research current advertising platforms and policies, prepare campaign hypotheses, analyze creative/message options, inspect measurement, and synthesize results.

Because ad platforms, interfaces, policies, attribution, and pricing change, execution must use current authoritative sources rather than relying only on Academy memory.

Founder owns budget, brand claims, risk tolerance, and scale decisions.
