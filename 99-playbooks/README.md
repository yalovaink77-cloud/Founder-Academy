# Playbooks

Playbooks are reusable operating procedures.

Unlike theory, a playbook describes exactly what should be done, what evidence should be collected, what decision belongs to the founder, and what counts as complete.

Every playbook should be:

- Executable.
- Measurable.
- Reusable across products.
- Evidence-driven.
- Compatible with the Founder AI Operating Protocol.

The objective is to reduce uncertainty and turn Founder Academy knowledge into repeatable business execution.

---

## Start a Real Project

Use:

- `project-startup-protocol.md`

This audits an idea or existing product against the Founder Academy lifecycle and finds the earliest consequential evidence gap.

Do not assume a technically finished product should start at launch.

## Core Founder Journey

### Opportunity and Validation

- `opportunity-validation-sprint.md`

Use to determine whether a real, meaningful, reachable problem justifies further investment.

### Customer Research

- `customer-research-sprint.md`

Use to understand observed customer behavior, goals, alternatives, triggers, objections, and language.

### Product Value

- `product-value-sprint.md`

Use to define the value hypothesis, first-value event, critical journey, MVP scope, and success evidence.

### Positioning and Pricing

- `positioning-pricing-sprint.md`

Use to define market position, alternatives, differentiated value, message hierarchy, pricing hypothesis, and pricing test.

### Beta and First Users

- `beta-first-users-sprint.md`

Use to test the product with relevant users, observe activation, collect feedback, and create first-user acquisition evidence.

### Go-To-Market and Distribution

- `go-to-market-distribution-sprint.md`

Use to define the initial market, customer journey, channel hypotheses, experiments, conversion path, and distribution scorecard.

### Product Launch

- `product-launch-sprint.md`

Use to run a controlled, measurable public release and produce a post-launch learning decision.

### Metrics and Revenue

- `metrics-revenue-sprint.md`

Use to create the funnel, metric dictionary, baselines, economic view, founder scorecard, and bottleneck diagnosis.

### Growth

- `growth-experiment-sprint.md`

Use to diagnose the current growth constraint and run focused, measurable experiments.

### Advertising Readiness

- `advertising-readiness-sprint.md`

Use to decide whether paid acquisition should begin and to bound the first paid experiment.

Advertising is treated as acceleration, not a substitute for validation, positioning, product value, retention, or measurement.

### Scaling

- `scaling-readiness-sprint.md`

Use to identify proven systems, bottlenecks, leverage mechanisms, guardrails, and the next scaling decision.

## Operating Rule

Use one active playbook at a time unless there is a specific reason for parallel work.

Each sprint should end with:

```text
Evidence
↓
Founder Decision
↓
Durable Record
↓
Next Logical Action
```

If a playbook produces activity but no interpretable evidence or decision, it is not working as intended.
