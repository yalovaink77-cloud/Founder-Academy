# Customer Research Sprint

## Purpose

Replace assumptions about customers with observed evidence about their goals, behavior, alternatives, language, and decision process.

## Sprint Question

> What must we understand about the customer before making product, positioning, pricing, or GTM decisions?

## Step 1 — Define the Learning Goal

State what is uncertain.

Examples:

- Who feels the problem most strongly?
- What triggers them to seek a solution?
- What alternative do they use today?
- What prevents adoption?
- What outcome matters most?

Do not begin with a long generic questionnaire.

## Step 2 — Select Participants

Recruit people who have relevant behavior or experience.

Prefer evidence of the problem over broad demographic similarity.

Record why each participant is relevant.

## Step 3 — Conduct Research

Ask about real events and behavior.

Explore:

- Situation and trigger.
- Desired outcome.
- Existing workflow.
- Alternative solutions.
- Friction and consequences.
- Purchase/adoption decision.
- Trust requirements.
- Natural words and phrases.

Avoid leading questions and premature pitching.

## Step 4 — Separate Observation From Interpretation

For each important note distinguish:

- What the person actually said or did.
- What the founder or AI infers from it.

This prevents interpretation from becoming false evidence.

## Step 5 — Synthesize Patterns

Group findings by repeated behavior rather than forcing everyone into one persona.

Look for:

- Repeated triggers.
- Repeated desired outcomes.
- Common alternatives.
- Common objections.
- High-intensity segments.
- Trust requirements.
- Language patterns.

Also record contradictions.

## Step 6 — Produce the Customer Model

Create:

- Primary customer/problem segment.
- Important secondary segments.
- Job or desired outcome.
- Trigger.
- Current alternative.
- Main friction.
- Decision criteria.
- Trust requirements.
- Vocabulary bank.

## Step 7 — Founder Decision

Decide whether the evidence is strong enough to:

- Narrow the target customer.
- Change the problem definition.
- Change the product hypothesis.
- Proceed to value/positioning work.
- Gather more evidence.

## Required Outputs

- Research objective.
- Participant/evidence set.
- Interview or observation notes.
- Pattern synthesis.
- Customer/problem segments.
- Customer decision journey.
- Vocabulary bank.
- Founder decision.

## Exit Criteria

The founder can explain the customer using evidence about behavior, not only demographics or imagined personas.

The founder knows:

- What triggers the problem.
- What customers do today.
- What outcome they want.
- What blocks change.
- How they evaluate alternatives.

## AI Role

AI may:

- Prepare research plans.
- Analyze notes.
- Find repeated patterns.
- Compare segments.
- Organize vocabulary.
- Highlight contradictions.

AI must not invent interviews, participants, quotes, or evidence.

## Durable Record

Preserve findings that affect future product, positioning, pricing, or distribution decisions, with evidence references where possible.
