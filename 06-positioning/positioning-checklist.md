# Positioning Checklist

Positioning is complete when:

* [ ] The target customer is clearly defined.
* [ ] The primary problem is identified.
* [ ] The value proposition is simple and clear.
* [ ] The positioning statement is complete.
* [ ] Competitors have been analyzed.
* [ ] A meaningful differentiator exists.
* [ ] Messaging is consistent across all channels.

Strong positioning allows customers to immediately understand why the product matters.
