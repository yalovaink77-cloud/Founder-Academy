# Vision

Founder Academy exists to solve one problem:

A founder can build products but still not know how to turn them into successful businesses.

The Academy is designed to become a permanent operating system for founders.

Its purpose is to provide repeatable systems for validating ideas, preparing products, finding users, launching, measuring, improving, generating revenue, and growing products.

Founder Academy is independent of any single project.

Every framework must be reusable across different products, markets, and business models.

The goal is not to launch one successful product.

The goal is to build a founder capable of launching successful products repeatedly.
