# Channel Evaluation — Research Report

## Purpose

Channel evaluation determines whether a distribution channel can reliably acquire valuable customers.

The objective is not maximum reach.

The objective is efficient, repeatable customer acquisition.

## Core Principles

Every channel is an experiment.

A channel should be evaluated by:

- Customer quality.
- Cost.
- Speed.
- Scalability.
- Sustainability.

## Channel Evaluation Framework

### Reach

Can the channel reach the target customer?

Questions:

- Are customers present?
- Is attention available?
- Can the audience be identified?

### Cost

Measure:

- Customer acquisition cost.
- Time investment.
- Required resources.

### Conversion

Measure:

- Clicks.
- Signups.
- Trials.
- Purchases.
- Sales.

### Quality

Measure:

- Activation.
- Retention.
- Revenue.
- Customer satisfaction.

### Scalability

Ask:

- Can this channel grow?
- Does cost remain sustainable?
- Does quality remain consistent?

## Channel Testing

Each experiment should define:

- Channel hypothesis.
- Target customer.
- Expected result.
- Budget/time limit.
- Success metric.
- Decision.

## Common Mistakes

- Choosing channels based on popularity.
- Optimizing traffic instead of customers.
- Ignoring customer quality.
- Scaling before economics work.

## Founder Academy Standard

A channel is validated when it repeatedly produces valuable customers at sustainable economics.
