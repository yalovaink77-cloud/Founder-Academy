# Founder Automation — Research Report

## Purpose

Founder automation describes the use of technology, systems, and processes to reduce repetitive work and increase execution capacity.

The objective is allowing founders to focus on high-value decisions and customer problems.

## Core Principles

Automation should:

- Remove repetitive tasks.
- Improve consistency.
- Reduce errors.
- Increase speed.
- Create leverage.

Automation should not replace understanding.

## Areas Of Automation

## Operations

- Notifications.
- Data processing.
- Reporting.

Examples:

- Email sequences.
- Content distribution.
- Campaign management.

## Customer Support

Examples:

- Knowledge bases.

- Routing systems.

## Product

Examples:

- Testing.
- Monitoring.
- Deployment processes.

## Knowledge Management

Examples:

- Document organization.
- Search systems.
- AI assistants.

## AI Automation

AI can support:

- Research.
- Summarization.
- Content creation.
- Data analysis.
- Decision preparation.

Human judgment remains responsible for important decisions.

## Automation Prioritization

Automate tasks that are:

- Repetitive.
- Time-consuming.
- Rule-based.
- Low strategic value.

Avoid automating:

- Core customer understanding.
- Important strategic decisions.
- Activities requiring human trust.

## Common Mistakes

- Automating broken processes.
- Adding complexity.
- Removing human feedback.
- Building systems nobody uses.

## Founder Academy Standard

Automation creates leverage when it increases the founder's ability to create customer value.
