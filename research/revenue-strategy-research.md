# Revenue Strategy — Research Report

## Purpose

Revenue strategy defines how a company creates, captures, and grows economic value.

The objective is building a sustainable business model.

## Core Principles

Revenue strategy connects:

- Customer value.
- Pricing.
- Revenue model.
- Acquisition.
- Retention.
- Expansion.

## Revenue Growth Paths

### Acquisition

Gain new customers.

Measure:

- New customers.
- Conversion.
- CAC.

### Retention

Keep existing customers.

Measure:

- Churn.
- Engagement.
- Renewal.

### Expansion

Increase customer value.

Examples:

- Upgrades.
- Additional usage.
- New products.

### Referral

Customers create new customers.

## Revenue Strategy Questions

Ask:

- Who pays?
- Why do they pay?
- How often?
- What causes renewal?
- What increases value?
- What causes churn?

## Unit Economics

Understand:

- Customer Acquisition Cost.
- Lifetime Value.
- Payback period.
- Gross margin.

## Common Mistakes

- Focusing only on acquisition.
- Ignoring retention.
- Underpricing.
- Scaling before economics work.
- Confusing revenue with profitability.

## Founder Academy Standard

Revenue growth should come from increasing customer value, not only increasing customer volume.
