# Dashboard — Research Report

## Purpose

A dashboard transforms important business data into a decision-making system.

The objective is not displaying more numbers.

The objective is helping founders understand what action should be taken.

## Core Principles

A good dashboard should:

- Focus on important decisions.
- Show trends.
- Highlight problems.
- Reduce uncertainty.
- Create accountability.

## Dashboard Layers

### Executive Layer

Answers:

- Is the business healthy?
- Is growth improving?
- Are risks increasing?

Metrics:

- Revenue
- Growth
- Retention
- Customer count
- Runway

### Product Layer

Answers:

- Are users receiving value?

Metrics:

- Activation
- Engagement
- Retention
- Feature usage

### Growth Layer

Answers:

- Are customers being acquired efficiently?

Metrics:

- Acquisition channels
- Conversion
- CAC
- LTV

### Operations Layer

Answers:

- Is execution improving?

Metrics:

- Support
- Reliability
- Delivery speed

## Dashboard Principles

Avoid:

- Too many metrics.
- Vanity metrics.
- Data without decisions.
- Unclear ownership.

Prefer:

- Few important metrics.
- Clear definitions.
- Regular review.

## Founder Academy Standard

A dashboard is a decision tool, not a reporting document.
