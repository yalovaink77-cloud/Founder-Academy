# Founder Academy v1 Roadmap — Research Report

## Purpose

Founder Academy v1 roadmap defines how the founder operating system moves from knowledge creation into practical company building.

The objective is transforming frameworks, research, and intelligence systems into repeatable founder execution.

## Phase 1 — Foundation

Goal:

Create founder clarity and operating principles.

Includes:

- Vision.
- Principles.
- Mental models.
- Founder OS.

Output:

A stable decision foundation.

## Phase 2 — Validation

Goal:

Determine whether opportunities are real.

Includes:

- Problem discovery.
- Customer research.
- Market analysis.
- Evidence collection.

Output:

Validated opportunity understanding.

## Phase 3 — Experimentation

Goal:

Learn quickly with minimal resources.

Includes:

- Hypothesis creation.
- MVP experiments.
- User testing.
- Feedback loops.

Output:

Evidence-based decisions.

## Phase 4 — Building

Goal:

Create products that solve validated problems.

Includes:

- MVP development.
- Product systems.
- Technical foundations.
- Quality standards.

Output:

Working product.

## Phase 5 — Market Entry

Goal:

Connect products with customers.

Includes:

- Positioning.
- Messaging.
- Distribution.
- Launch.

Output:

Initial traction.

## Phase 6 — Growth

Goal:

Create repeatable expansion.

Includes:

- Acquisition.
- Activation.
- Retention.
- Referral.

Output:

Growth system.

## Phase 7 — Revenue

Goal:

Create sustainable economics.

Includes:

- Pricing.
- Business model.
- Revenue optimization.
- Financial intelligence.

Output:

Sustainable business.

## Phase 8 — Scaling

Goal:

Increase company capability.

Includes:

- Operations.
- Organization.
- Automation.
- Capital allocation.

Output:

Scalable company.

## Application Model

Every project follows:

Idea
↓
Research
↓
Validation
↓
Experiment
↓
Build
↓
Launch
↓
Measure
↓
Improve
↓
Scale

## Project Integration

Founder Academy can be applied to:

- Lumora.
- PiercingConnect.
- Kuryele.
- Future projects.

Each project becomes a practical learning environment.

## Founder Academy Standard

Knowledge is valuable only when converted into decisions, actions, and measurable outcomes.
