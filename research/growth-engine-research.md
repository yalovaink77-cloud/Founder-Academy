# Growth Engine — Research Report

## Purpose

A growth engine defines the repeatable system that allows a company to acquire, activate, retain, and expand customers.

The objective is sustainable growth.

## Core Principles

Growth is not a single tactic.

It is a system connecting:

- Product value.
- Distribution.
- Customer behaviour.
- Retention.
- Revenue.

## Growth Engine Types

## Sticky Growth Engine

Focus:

Retention.

Works when:

- Product creates habits.
- Customers receive ongoing value.
- Switching becomes difficult.

Metrics:

- Retention.
- Churn.
- Engagement.

## Viral Growth Engine

Focus:

User-driven acquisition.

Works when:

- Users naturally invite others.
- Sharing increases value.

Metrics:

- Referral rate.
- Viral coefficient.

## Paid Growth Engine

Focus:

Buying customers through channels.

Works when:

- LTV exceeds CAC.
- Acquisition is predictable.

Metrics:

- CAC.
- Conversion.
- Payback period.

## Content Growth Engine

Focus:

Creating valuable content that attracts customers.

Examples:

- SEO.
- Education.
- Community.

Metrics:

- Organic traffic.
- Leads.
- Conversion.

## Growth Engine Evaluation

Ask:

- Is it repeatable?
- Is it measurable?
- Is it scalable?
- Does economics work?
- Does customer value increase?

## Common Mistakes

- Chasing growth before retention.
- Using tactics without a system.
- Ignoring customer value.
- Scaling broken funnels.

## Founder Academy Standard

Growth engines amplify existing customer value.

They cannot replace product-market fit.
