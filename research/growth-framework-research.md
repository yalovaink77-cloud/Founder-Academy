# Growth Framework — Research Report

## Purpose

A growth framework provides a structured system for turning product value into sustainable business growth.

The objective is creating repeatable improvement, not isolated tactics.

## Core Principles

Growth depends on:

- Product value.
- Distribution.
- Customer behaviour.
- Retention.
- Revenue.

Growth cannot compensate for a product customers do not value.

## Growth Framework

## 1. Understand

Identify:

- Target customers.
- Customer problems.
- Desired outcomes.
- Current alternatives.

## 2. Measure

Define:

- North Star Metric.
- Funnel metrics.
- Business metrics.

## 3. Experiment

Test:

- Acquisition.
- Activation.
- Retention.
- Revenue.
- Referral.

## 4. Learn

Analyze:

- What worked?
- What failed?
- Why?

## 5. Scale

Increase investment only after repeatability exists.

## Growth Areas

### Acquisition

How customers discover the product.

### Activation

How quickly customers experience value.

### Retention

Why customers continue.

### Revenue

How value becomes sustainable income.

### Referral

How customers create new growth.

## Common Mistakes

- Scaling before retention.
- Chasing competitors.
- Optimizing vanity metrics.
- Running random experiments.
- Ignoring customer value.

## Founder Academy Standard

Growth is a continuous learning system that compounds customer value.
