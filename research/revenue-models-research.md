# Revenue Models — Research Report

## Purpose

A revenue model defines how a company converts customer value into sustainable income.

The objective is creating a business model where customer success and company success are aligned.

## Core Principles

A strong revenue model should:

- Match customer value creation.
- Be understandable.
- Scale with usage or success.
- Support sustainability.
- Encourage retention.

## Revenue Model Types

### Subscription

Recurring payments.

Suitable for:

- SaaS.
- Memberships.
- Continuous services.

Advantages:

- Predictable revenue.
- Customer relationship.

Challenges:

- Retention requirement.
- Churn management.

### Transaction

Revenue from individual purchases.

Suitable for:

- Marketplaces.
- Commerce.
- Services.

### Usage-Based

Payment increases with consumption.

Suitable for:

- APIs.
- Infrastructure.
- AI services.

### Freemium

Free entry with paid upgrades.

Works when:

- Free users create distribution.
- Premium value is clear.

### Marketplace Commission

Platform takes a percentage of transactions.

Requires:

- Supply.
- Demand.
- Trust.

### Advertising

Revenue from audience attention.

Requires:

- Scale.
- Engagement.
- Valuable audience.

## Revenue Validation

Test:

- Will customers pay?
- How much?
- How often?
- Why continue?
- What causes churn?

## Metrics

Track:

- Revenue.
- MRR.
- ARR.
- ARPU.
- LTV.
- Churn.
- Expansion revenue.

## Common Mistakes

- Choosing revenue model before understanding customers.
- Optimizing revenue before value.
- Ignoring retention.
- Copying competitors blindly.

## Founder Academy Standard

The best revenue model aligns customer value with company growth.
