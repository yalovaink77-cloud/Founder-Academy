# Founder Strategy — Research Report

## Purpose

Founder strategy defines how founders choose where to compete, how to create value, and how to allocate limited resources.

The objective is building a clear path from current position to long-term advantage.

## Core Principles

Strategy is about choices.

A strategy requires:

- Clear goals.
- Understanding of the market.
- Priorities.
- Trade-offs.
- Coordinated actions.

## Strategy Components

## Market Choice

Define:

- Which customers to serve.
- Which problems to solve.
- Which markets to avoid.

## Value Creation

Understand:

- Why customers choose the product.
- What advantage is created.
- How value improves over time.

## Competitive Position

Define:

- How the company differs.
- Why competitors cannot easily replace it.

## Resource Allocation

Decide:

- Where to invest.
- What to ignore.
- What capabilities to build.

## Strategic Trade-offs

Good strategy requires saying no.

Examples:

- Not serving every customer.
- Not building every feature.
- Not entering every market.

## Strategy Process

1. Understand current position.
2. Identify opportunities.
3. Define choices.
4. Allocate resources.
5. Execute.
6. Measure.
7. Adapt.

## Common Mistakes

- Confusing goals with strategy.
- Following competitors blindly.
- Trying to serve everyone.
- Changing direction constantly.
- Ignoring trade-offs.

## Founder Academy Standard

Strategy is choosing what to do and what not to do to create lasting advantage.
