# Beta Testing — Research Report

## Purpose

Beta testing validates whether a product works effectively with real users before wider release.

The objective is learning, quality improvement, and risk reduction.

## Core Principles

Beta users are not only testers.

They are:

- Early customers.
- Learning partners.
- Sources of behavioural evidence.
- Sources of product improvement.

## Beta Objectives

Validate:

- Product usefulness.
- User experience.
- Reliability.
- Onboarding.
- Retention.
- Support requirements.

## Beta Types

### Closed Beta

Limited users.

Purpose:

- Deep feedback.
- Controlled testing.
- Finding major issues.

### Open Beta

Larger public access.

Purpose:

- Wider feedback.
- More usage patterns.
- Infrastructure testing.

## Selecting Beta Users

Good beta users:

- Match target customer.
- Experience the problem.
- Provide detailed feedback.
- Use the product realistically.

Avoid selecting only:

- Friends.
- Supportive people.
- Non-target users.

## Feedback System

Collect:

- Problems encountered.
- Confusion points.
- Feature usage.
- Requests.
- Retention behaviour.
- Satisfaction.

## Metrics

Measure:

- Activation.
- Usage frequency.
- Retention.
- Errors.
- Support requests.
- Conversion.

## Common Mistakes

- Too many users too early.
- No feedback process.
- Ignoring negative feedback.
- Treating all requests equally.
- Measuring only bugs.

## Founder Academy Standard

Beta testing is a structured learning phase, not simply a preview release.
