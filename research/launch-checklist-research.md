# Launch Checklist — Research Report

## Purpose

A launch checklist ensures that product, market, operational, and measurement systems are prepared before release.

## Product Readiness

Confirm:

- Core user problem is validated.
- Critical workflows work.
- Major bugs are resolved.
- User onboarding is ready.
- Support process exists.

## Market Readiness

Confirm:

- Target customer defined.
- Positioning clear.
- Value proposition tested.
- Pricing ready.
- Launch message prepared.

## Distribution Readiness

Confirm:

- Channels selected.
- Audience identified.
- Content prepared.
- Partnerships ready.
- Acquisition tracking installed.

## Measurement Readiness

Track:

- Acquisition.
- Activation.
- Retention.
- Conversion.
- Revenue.
- Feedback.

## Operational Readiness

Prepare:

- Customer support.
- Documentation.
- Monitoring.
- Incident response.

## Common Mistakes

- Launching without analytics.
- Ignoring onboarding.
- Treating launch day as success measurement.
- Focusing only on traffic.
- No post-launch plan.

## Founder Academy Standard

A launch is successful when it creates measurable customer learning and business progress.
