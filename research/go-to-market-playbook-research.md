# Go-To-Market Playbook — Research Report

## Purpose

A Go-To-Market playbook converts strategy into repeatable execution.

It defines how a product moves from validated idea to customer adoption.

## Core Principles

GTM should connect:

- Target customer
- Problem
- Positioning
- Channel
- Sales process
- Pricing
- Retention

## GTM Preparation

Before launch:

- Customer segment defined.
- Problem validated.
- Solution validated.
- Positioning created.
- Pricing tested.
- Distribution channels selected.

## Launch Process

### Awareness

Create discovery through:

- Content
- Community
- Partnerships
- Outreach
- Paid channels

### Acquisition

Convert attention into:

- Leads
- Trials
- Demos
- Purchases

### Activation

Deliver first customer value quickly.

### Retention

Ensure customers continue receiving value.

### Referral

Create reasons for customers to recommend.

## Execution Rhythm

Review:

- Customer feedback
- Conversion data
- Acquisition channels
- Retention
- Revenue

Adjust based on evidence.

## Common Mistakes

- Launching without validation.
- Focusing only on acquisition.
- Ignoring onboarding.
- Scaling before retention.
- Copying competitor launches.

## Founder Academy Standard

A GTM playbook is a learning system that improves through customer evidence.
