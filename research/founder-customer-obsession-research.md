# Founder Customer Obsession — Research Report

## Purpose

Founder customer obsession describes the discipline of deeply understanding customers and making customer value the foundation of company decisions.

The objective is building products and businesses around real customer needs.

## Core Principles

Customer obsession means:

- Listen before building.
- Observe behaviour, not only opinions.
- Solve important problems.
- Continuously improve customer outcomes.

## Customer Understanding

Founders should understand:

- Who the customer is.
- What problem they experience.
- How they solve it today.
- Why current solutions are insufficient.
- What success means for them.

## Customer Discovery

Methods:

- Interviews.
- Observation.
- Surveys.
- Usage analysis.
- Support conversations.

The goal is discovering reality, not confirming assumptions.

## Customer Feedback

Good feedback helps identify:

- Problems.
- Confusion.
- Opportunities.
- Missing value.

Feedback should guide decisions, not create uncontrolled feature lists.

## Customer Metrics

Important signals:

- Retention.
- Satisfaction.
- Repeat usage.
- Referrals.
- Revenue.

## Common Mistakes

- Building from founder assumptions.
- Ignoring negative feedback.
- Listening only to existing customers.
- Optimizing internal goals over customer outcomes.

## Founder Academy Standard

The customer is the source of truth.

Strong companies are built by understanding and repeatedly improving customer outcomes.
