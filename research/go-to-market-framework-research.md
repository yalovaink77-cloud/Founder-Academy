# Go-To-Market Framework — Research Report

## Purpose

Go-To-Market (GTM) defines how a product reaches the right customers, communicates value, converts demand, and creates sustainable growth.

## Core Principles

A GTM strategy connects:

- Customer
- Problem
- Positioning
- Distribution
- Sales
- Pricing
- Retention

## GTM Components

### Target Customer

Define:

- Who buys.
- Who uses.
- Who influences.
- Where they can be reached.

### Positioning

Explain:

- Why this product.
- Why now.
- Why better.

### Distribution

Identify:

- Acquisition channels.
- Sales channels.
- Partnerships.
- Community.

### Conversion

Understand:

- Customer journey.
- Buying process.
- Objections.
- Trust requirements.

### Retention

Ensure:

- Continued value.
- Habit formation.
- Customer success.

## Channel Selection

Evaluate channels by:

- Reachability.
- Cost.
- Speed.
- Scalability.
- Competition.

## Common Mistakes

- Trying every channel.
- Scaling before product-market fit.
- Ignoring retention.
- Copying competitors.
- Confusing traffic with customers.

## Founder Academy Standard

A GTM strategy is a repeatable system for acquiring and retaining valuable customers.
