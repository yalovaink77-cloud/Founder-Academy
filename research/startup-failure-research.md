# Startup Failure — Research Report

## Purpose

Understanding startup failure helps founders identify risks before investing significant time and resources.

The objective is not avoiding all failure.

The objective is learning faster and reducing avoidable mistakes.

## Core Failure Patterns

## 1. No Real Customer Need

The product solves a problem customers do not consider important.

Signals:

- Low usage.
- Weak retention.
- No urgency.
- No payment.

## 2. Running Out Of Money

Causes:

- Excessive spending.
- Scaling too early.
- Weak revenue model.
- Poor planning.

## 3. Poor Product-Market Fit

Symptoms:

- Customers do not stay.
- Growth requires constant effort.
- Users do not recommend.

## 4. Wrong Timing

A product may fail because:

- Market is not ready.
- Technology is immature.
- Customer behaviour has not changed.

## 5. Weak Execution

Examples:

- Slow learning.
- Poor prioritization.
- Lack of focus.

## 6. Ignoring Competition Or Alternatives

Customers compare solutions.

The alternative may be:

- Competitor.
- Manual process.
- Doing nothing.

## Prevention

Founders should:

- Validate problems early.
- Measure retention.
- Preserve cash.
- Talk to customers.
- Run experiments.
- Prioritize learning.

## Founder Academy Standard

Startup failure is often caused by insufficient evidence before commitment.
