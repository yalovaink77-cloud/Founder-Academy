# Product-Market Fit — Research Report

## Purpose

Product-Market Fit (PMF) exists when a product consistently creates enough value that customers adopt it, continue using it, recommend it, and willingly pay for it.

PMF is an outcome, not a launch milestone.

## Core Principles

Product-Market Fit cannot be declared.

It must emerge from customer behaviour.

## PMF Signals

Strong indicators include:

- High retention
- Repeat usage
- Organic referrals
- Low churn
- Revenue growth
- Customer disappointment if the product disappears
- Expanding usage

## Weak Signals

The following do not prove Product-Market Fit:

- Downloads
- Press coverage
- Social media engagement
- Waiting lists
- Investor interest
- Traffic

## Measurement

Measure:

- Activation
- Retention
- Churn
- Engagement
- Referral
- Conversion
- Revenue
- Customer satisfaction

## Superhuman Test

Ask:

"How would you feel if you could no longer use this product?"

Answers:

- Very disappointed
- Somewhat disappointed
- Not disappointed

A high percentage of "Very disappointed" users is a strong PMF signal.

## Common Mistakes

- Scaling before retention.
- Measuring vanity metrics.
- Ignoring churn.
- Assuming launch equals PMF.
- Confusing growth with product quality.

## Founder Academy Standard

PMF exists when customers repeatedly demonstrate that the product delivers meaningful value through continued behaviour.
