# Launch Strategy — Research Report

## Purpose

Launch strategy defines how a product moves from development into the market while maximizing learning and reducing risk.

A launch is not the finish line.

It is the beginning of market feedback.

## Core Principles

A successful launch requires:

- Clear target customer.
- Clear value proposition.
- Distribution channel.
- Measurement system.
- Feedback process.

## Launch Types

### Private Launch

Limited users.

Purpose:

- Validate.
- Discover problems.
- Improve.

### Beta Launch

Controlled public testing.

Purpose:

- Increase usage.
- Collect feedback.
- Improve reliability.

### Public Launch

Broad availability.

Purpose:

- Acquire customers.
- Grow distribution.

## Before Launch

Confirm:

- Problem validated.
- Solution validated.
- Positioning ready.
- Pricing ready.
- Support ready.
- Analytics ready.

## Launch Metrics

Measure:

- Acquisition.
- Activation.
- Retention.
- Conversion.
- Revenue.
- Customer feedback.

## Launch Channels

Examples:

- Existing audience.
- Communities.
- Content.
- Partnerships.
- Product Hunt.
- Press.
- Paid channels.

## Common Mistakes

- Launching without users.
- Treating launch as marketing only.
- Ignoring onboarding.
- Measuring attention instead of customers.
- Scaling before retention.

## Founder Academy Standard

A launch is an experiment that begins a continuous improvement cycle.
