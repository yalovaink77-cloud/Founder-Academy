# Unit Economics — Research Report

## Purpose

Unit economics measures whether each customer relationship creates sustainable economic value.

The objective is understanding whether growth creates or destroys value.

## Core Principles

A business should understand:

- Cost to acquire a customer.
- Value generated by a customer.
- Time required to recover acquisition cost.

## Core Metrics

## Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)

The total cost required to acquire one customer.

Includes:

- Marketing costs.
- Sales costs.
- Campaign expenses.

Formula:

CAC = Total Acquisition Cost / Number of New Customers

## Lifetime Value (LTV)

The estimated economic value generated by a customer during the relationship.

Depends on:

- Revenue.
- Margin.
- Retention.
- Purchase frequency.

## LTV:CAC Ratio

Compares customer value with acquisition cost.

A healthy ratio indicates sustainable growth.

## Payback Period

Measures how long it takes to recover acquisition cost.

Shorter payback improves cash efficiency.

## Gross Margin

Revenue remaining after direct costs.

Important for understanding scalability.

## Validation

Track:

- CAC by channel.
- LTV by segment.
- Churn.
- Retention.
- Expansion.

## Common Mistakes

- Growing customers without economics.
- Ignoring retention.
- Measuring revenue without costs.
- Assuming all customers have equal value.

## Founder Academy Standard

Growth is valuable only when customer economics are sustainable.
