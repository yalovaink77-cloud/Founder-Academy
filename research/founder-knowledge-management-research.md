# Founder Knowledge Management — Research Report

## Purpose

Founder knowledge management defines how founders capture, organize, retrieve, and apply knowledge to improve company decisions.

The objective is turning information into a long-term strategic asset.

## Core Principles

Knowledge management requires:

- Capture.
- Organization.
- Retrieval.
- Application.
- Improvement.

Information becomes valuable when it improves decisions.

## Knowledge Sources

Important sources:

- Customer conversations.
- Market research.
- Experiments.
- Product data.
- Strategic decisions.
- Industry knowledge.

## Documentation

Founders should document:

- Assumptions.
- Decisions.
- Experiments.
- Lessons learned.
- Important context.

## Decision Records

Useful records include:

- What was decided.
- Why it was decided.
- Alternatives considered.
- Expected outcome.
- Actual result.

## Knowledge Systems

A useful system should provide:

- Easy capture.
- Clear structure.
- Fast retrieval.
- Continuous updates.

## AI And Knowledge Management

AI systems can help with:

- Searching knowledge.
- Summarizing information.
- Finding patterns.
- Supporting decisions.

Human judgment remains responsible for final decisions.

## Common Mistakes

- Collecting information without structure.
- Losing important decisions.
- Creating documentation nobody uses.
- Repeating solved problems.

## Founder Academy Standard

A founder's accumulated knowledge should become a reusable company asset.
