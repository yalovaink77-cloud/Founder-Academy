# Analytics Checklist — Research Report

## Purpose

An analytics checklist ensures that measurement systems provide reliable information for product and business decisions.

## Foundation

Confirm:

- Business goals defined.
- Key questions identified.
- Metrics selected.
- Metric definitions documented.

## Tracking

Confirm:

- Important user actions tracked.
- Events named consistently.
- Data quality verified.
- Tracking tested.

## Metrics

Monitor:

### Acquisition

- Traffic sources.
- Conversion.
- Customer acquisition cost.

### Activation

- First value event.
- Onboarding completion.

### Retention

- Returning users.
- Churn.
- Usage frequency.

### Revenue

- Payments.
- Conversion.
- Lifetime value.

### Referral

- Invitations.
- Recommendations.

## Review Process

Define:

- Review frequency.
- Metric owners.
- Decision process.
- Action items.

## Common Mistakes

- Collecting data without purpose.
- Tracking vanity metrics.
- Ignoring retention.
- No metric ownership.

## Founder Academy Standard

Analytics should create decisions, not dashboards full of numbers.
