# Founder Mission — Research Report

## Purpose

Founder mission defines the deeper reason a company exists beyond creating a product or generating revenue.

The objective is creating long-term direction and alignment.

## Core Principles

A strong mission connects:

- Customer problem.
- Desired change.
- Company purpose.
- Long-term impact.

## Mission Components

## Customer

Who benefits?

## Problem

What important issue is being solved?

## Change

What improvement should exist in the world?

## Direction

What future is the company working toward?

## Mission vs Vision

Mission:

- Why the company exists.
- What it does today.

Vision:

- The future state the company wants to create.

## Benefits

A clear mission helps:

- Prioritization.
- Team alignment.
- Customer trust.
- Long-term decisions.

## Common Mistakes

- Creating generic statements.
- Making the company the hero.
- Using mission only for marketing.
- Ignoring customer value.

## Founder Academy Standard

A mission should guide decisions, not only describe intentions.
