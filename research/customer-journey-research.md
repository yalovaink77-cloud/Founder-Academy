# Customer Journey — Research Report

## Purpose

Customer Journey maps the complete experience from problem awareness to long-term retention.

The objective is to identify friction, opportunities, and improvement points throughout the customer lifecycle.

## Journey Stages

1. Problem Awareness
2. Solution Discovery
3. Evaluation
4. Decision
5. Onboarding
6. First Success
7. Regular Usage
8. Retention
9. Referral

## Questions

At every stage identify:

- Customer goal
- Customer actions
- Customer thoughts
- Customer emotions
- Friction
- Opportunities

## Awareness

Questions:

- How does the customer discover the problem?
- What triggers the search?

## Discovery

Questions:

- Which channels are used?
- Which competitors are evaluated?

## Decision

Questions:

- Why buy?
- Why not buy?
- What creates trust?
- What creates doubt?

## Onboarding

Questions:

- How quickly is value delivered?
- Where do users stop?

## Retention

Questions:

- Why do users return?
- Why do users leave?

## Referral

Questions:

- Why do customers recommend the product?
- What motivates sharing?

## Common Mistakes

- Ignoring post-purchase experience.
- Optimizing acquisition only.
- Ignoring emotional journey.
- Assuming a linear process.

## Founder Academy Standard

Every important customer journey stage should be measurable and continuously improved.
