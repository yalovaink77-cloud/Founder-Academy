# Pricing Models — Research Report

## Purpose

Pricing models define how revenue is collected from customers.

The correct model should align customer success with business success.

## Common Pricing Models

### Subscription

Recurring monthly or annual payments.

Best for:

- SaaS
- Productivity
- AI
- Communities

### One-Time Purchase

Single payment.

Best for:

- Software
- Templates
- Courses

### Usage-Based

Customers pay for actual usage.

Examples:

- API requests
- Storage
- Compute
- Messages

### Tiered Pricing

Different plans for different customer needs.

Typical:

- Free
- Starter
- Pro
- Business
- Enterprise

### Freemium

Core product is free.

Advanced value requires payment.

### Marketplace

Platform earns commission per transaction.

### Enterprise

Custom pricing.

Usually includes:

- Sales
- Negotiation
- Contracts

## Evaluation Criteria

Evaluate:

- Customer value
- Revenue predictability
- Scalability
- Simplicity
- Expansion potential
- Sales complexity

## Validation

Test:

- Conversion
- Expansion
- Retention
- Churn
- Average Revenue Per User
- Lifetime Value

## Common Mistakes

- Too many plans.
- Confusing pricing.
- Charging before value.
- Giving away premium value.
- Never reviewing pricing.

## Founder Academy Standard

Pricing models should grow with customer success.
