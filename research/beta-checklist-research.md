# Beta Testing Checklist — Research Report

## Purpose

A beta checklist ensures that product testing produces reliable learning before wider release.

## Preparation

Confirm:

- Target users defined.
- Beta goals defined.
- Testing period defined.
- Feedback channels prepared.
- Metrics selected.

## User Selection

Check:

- Users match target segment.
- Users experience the problem.
- Users can provide useful feedback.
- Users understand beta expectations.

## Onboarding

Prepare:

- Clear instructions.
- Account setup.
- First success path.
- Support process.

## Testing

Monitor:

- Usage.
- Errors.
- Confusion points.
- Retention.
- Feedback.

## Feedback

Collect:

- Problems.
- Requests.
- Bugs.
- Satisfaction.
- Behaviour patterns.

## Evaluation

Measure:

- Activation.
- Retention.
- Engagement.
- Conversion.
- Product quality.

## Decision

After beta:

- Continue.
- Improve.
- Pivot.
- Stop.

## Common Mistakes

- Launching without goals.
- Collecting feedback without analysis.
- Ignoring inactive users.
- Treating beta as marketing only.

## Founder Academy Standard

A beta phase is successful when it produces evidence for better product decisions.
