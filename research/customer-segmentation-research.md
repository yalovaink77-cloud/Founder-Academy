# Customer Segmentation — Research Report

## Purpose

Customer segmentation identifies the smallest group of customers with the strongest need for the problem being solved.

The objective is focus.

## Core Principles

- Start narrow.
- Expand later.
- Different segments have different problems.
- Different segments buy for different reasons.
- Different segments require different positioning.

## Segmentation Dimensions

Possible dimensions:

- Industry
- Job role
- Company size
- Geography
- Demographics
- Behaviour
- Goals
- Problems
- Buying power
- Technology adoption

## Early Adopters

Early adopters usually:

- Already feel the problem.
- Already seek alternatives.
- Already spend time solving it.
- Accept imperfect products.
- Provide useful feedback.

## Segment Evaluation

Evaluate each segment by:

- Problem severity
- Frequency
- Reachability
- Ability to pay
- Willingness to change
- Competition
- Market size

## Common Mistakes

- Targeting everyone.
- Segmenting only by age or gender.
- Ignoring buying authority.
- Choosing the largest market first.
- Changing segments too frequently.

## Founder Academy Standard

The first target market should be the smallest viable group with the strongest pain.
