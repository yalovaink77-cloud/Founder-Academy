# Channel Selection — Research Report

## Purpose

Channel selection determines where and how a product reaches customers efficiently.

The objective is not to use every channel.

The objective is to find channels that can repeatedly reach valuable customers.

## Core Principles

A channel should be evaluated by:

- Customer fit
- Cost
- Speed
- Scalability
- Sustainability
- Learning value

## Channel Categories

### Owned Channels

Controlled by the company:

- Website
- Email list
- Community
- Content library

Advantages:

- Long-term asset
- Direct relationship

### Earned Channels

Generated through reputation:

- Word of mouth
- Media
- Community sharing
- Referrals

Advantages:

- Trust
- Lower acquisition cost

### Paid Channels

Examples:

- Advertising
- Sponsorships
- Influencer partnerships

Advantages:

- Speed
- Scale

Requires:

- Strong conversion
- Good economics

### Partner Channels

Examples:

- Integrations
- Affiliates
- Strategic partnerships

Advantages:

- Access to existing audiences

## Channel Testing

Every channel should have:

- Hypothesis
- Target audience
- Experiment
- Cost
- Expected result
- Success metric

## Channel Metrics

Measure:

- Customer acquisition cost
- Conversion rate
- Activation
- Retention
- Revenue
- Payback period

## Common Mistakes

- Testing too many channels.
- Optimizing traffic instead of customers.
- Scaling before economics work.
- Copying competitors blindly.

## Founder Academy Standard

The best channel is the one that creates repeatable customer acquisition with sustainable economics.
