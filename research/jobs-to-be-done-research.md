# Jobs To Be Done — Research Report

## Purpose

Customers do not buy products.

They hire products to make progress in a specific situation.

The objective is to understand the progress customers seek.

## Core Principles

Customers hire products to:

- Solve problems.
- Reduce effort.
- Save time.
- Reduce risk.
- Increase status.
- Create enjoyment.
- Achieve personal or business goals.

## Job Types

### Functional Job

Complete a practical task.

### Emotional Job

Reduce anxiety, frustration, uncertainty, or stress.

### Social Job

Improve reputation, identity, or belonging.

Most successful products solve multiple job types.

## Hiring Criteria

Customers compare:

- Current solution
- Switching cost
- Risk
- Price
- Trust
- Expected outcome

Products compete against existing behaviour, not only competitors.

## Forces Of Progress

Customers change when:

- The current situation creates pain.
- A better future is attractive.
- Anxiety about change is manageable.
- Existing habits are weak enough to break.

## Interview Focus

Avoid asking:

"What features do you want?"

Instead ask:

- What were you trying to accomplish?
- What happened?
- Why did you choose that solution?
- What almost stopped you?
- What outcome mattered most?

## Common Mistakes

- Confusing demographics with jobs.
- Assuming competitors are only similar products.
- Ignoring emotional motivations.
- Ignoring switching costs.

## Founder Academy Standard

Products should be designed around customer progress rather than product features.
