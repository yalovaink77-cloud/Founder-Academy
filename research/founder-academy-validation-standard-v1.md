# Founder Academy — Validation Standard v1

## Purpose

This document defines the official Founder Academy validation standard by synthesizing:

- The Mom Test
- Steve Blank
- The Lean Startup
- Y Combinator
- Paul Graham
- Airbnb
- Dropbox
- Buffer
- Superhuman

## Validation Pipeline

1. Identify the problem.
2. Define assumptions.
3. Rank assumptions by risk.
4. Talk to real customers.
5. Observe behaviour.
6. Test the smallest valid experiment.
7. Collect behavioural evidence.
8. Collect commitment evidence.
9. Collect economic evidence.
10. Decide:
    - Continue
    - Iterate
    - Pivot
    - Stop

## Evidence Hierarchy

Level 1

Opinion

↓

Level 2

Past Behaviour

↓

Level 3

Commitment

↓

Level 4

Payment

Validation confidence increases as evidence moves upward.

## Founder Rules

Founders should:

- Talk directly with customers.
- Test assumptions before building.
- Separate problem validation from solution validation.
- Build the smallest useful experiment.
- Measure behaviour instead of opinions.
- Preserve cash while searching.
- Delay scaling until repeatability exists.

## Validation Principles

Never validate with:

- compliments,
- likes,
- downloads,
- page views,
- followers,
- vanity metrics.

Prefer:

- repeat behaviour,
- retention,
- referrals,
- commitments,
- payments.

## MVP Standard

Every MVP must answer one important business question.

An MVP without a learning objective is incomplete.

## Product-Market Fit

Product-Market Fit is not:

- launch,
- downloads,
- publicity,
- signups.

Product-Market Fit requires evidence of:

- retention,
- repeat usage,
- customer satisfaction,
- willingness to pay,
- organic recommendation.

## Scaling Rule

Scale only after:

- repeatable acquisition,
- repeatable activation,
- repeatable retention,
- repeatable revenue.

## Founder Academy Principle

Every important business decision should be supported by multiple forms of evidence rather than assumptions.
