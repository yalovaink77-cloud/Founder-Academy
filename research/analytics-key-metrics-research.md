# Key Metrics — Research Report

## Purpose

Key metrics identify the measurements that show whether a product creates customer value and whether the business can grow sustainably.

The objective is focus.

## Core Principles

A metric is valuable when it:

- Measures meaningful behaviour.
- Supports a decision.
- Connects to business goals.
- Can be improved through action.

## Metric Categories

## Acquisition

Measures how customers discover the product.

Examples:

- Visitors.
- Leads.
- Signups.
- Customer acquisition cost.

## Activation

Measures whether users experience initial value.

Examples:

- Completed onboarding.
- First successful action.
- Time to value.

## Retention

Measures whether users continue receiving value.

Examples:

- Returning users.
- Active users.
- Churn.
- Usage frequency.

## Revenue

Measures business sustainability.

Examples:

- Revenue.
- Conversion.
- Average revenue per user.
- Lifetime value.

## Referral

Measures organic growth.

Examples:

- Invitations.
- Sharing.
- Recommendations.

## North Star Metric

A North Star Metric represents the core value delivered to customers.

It should:

- Reflect customer value.
- Predict business growth.
- Guide team decisions.

## Common Mistakes

- Tracking too many metrics.
- Optimizing vanity metrics.
- Ignoring retention.
- Measuring activity instead of value.

## Founder Academy Standard

The best metrics explain whether customers receive lasting value.
