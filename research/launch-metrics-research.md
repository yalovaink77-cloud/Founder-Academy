# Launch Metrics — Research Report

## Purpose

Launch metrics determine whether a product release creates meaningful customer and business progress.

The objective is measuring behaviour, not attention.

## Core Principles

Metrics should answer:

- Did customers discover us?
- Did they understand the value?
- Did they experience success?
- Did they continue?
- Did they pay?
- Did they recommend?

## Funnel Metrics

### Acquisition

Measures how users arrive.

Examples:

- Visitors
- Signups
- Leads
- Downloads

### Activation

Measures first value.

Examples:

- Completed onboarding
- First successful action
- First meaningful outcome

### Retention

Measures continued value.

Examples:

- Returning users
- Active users
- Repeat purchases

### Revenue

Measures business value.

Examples:

- Conversion
- Subscription
- Revenue
- Expansion

### Referral

Measures organic growth.

Examples:

- Invitations
- Recommendations
- Sharing

## Avoid Vanity Metrics

Weak indicators:

- Impressions
- Followers
- Traffic alone
- Downloads alone
- Media mentions

Strong indicators:

- Activation
- Retention
- Payment
- Referral

## Metric Selection

Every launch should define:

- Primary goal.
- Key metric.
- Success threshold.
- Failure threshold.
- Decision.

## Common Mistakes

- Tracking everything without decisions.
- Optimizing acquisition before retention.
- Celebrating numbers without customer value.
- Ignoring churn.

## Founder Academy Standard

The best launch metrics measure whether customers receive repeated value.
