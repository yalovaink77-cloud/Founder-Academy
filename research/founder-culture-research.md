# Founder Culture — Research Report

## Purpose

Founder culture defines the principles, behaviours, and standards that guide how a company operates.

The objective is creating consistent decision-making and execution.

## Core Principles

Culture is created by:

- What founders reward.
- What founders tolerate.
- How decisions are made.
- How problems are handled.

Culture is behaviour, not slogans.

## Culture Components

## Values

Define:

- What matters.
- What behaviours are expected.
- What decisions are encouraged.

## Standards

Define:

- Quality expectations.
- Communication rules.
- Execution discipline.

## Decision Principles

Define:

- How trade-offs are made.
- How uncertainty is handled.
- How priorities are chosen.

## Learning Culture

Strong startup cultures encourage:

- Experimentation.
- Feedback.
- Honest communication.
- Learning from mistakes.

## Customer Focus

Healthy cultures prioritize:

- Customer value.
- Evidence.
- Real problems.

## Common Mistakes

- Writing values without changing behaviour.
- Ignoring bad habits.
- Rewarding speed over quality.
- Avoiding difficult feedback.

## Founder Academy Standard

Culture is an operating system for behaviour and decisions.
