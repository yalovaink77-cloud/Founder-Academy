# Founder Product Thinking — Research Report

## Purpose

Founder product thinking defines how founders understand customers, problems, solutions, and product decisions.

The objective is building products that create meaningful customer value.

## Core Principles

Great products start with:

- Customer problems.
- User behaviour.
- Clear value.
- Continuous learning.

A product is not a collection of features.

It is a system that helps customers achieve outcomes.

## Problem First Thinking

Before building:

Understand:

- Who has the problem.
- How painful it is.
- What alternatives exist.
- Why current solutions fail.

## Customer Understanding

Use:

- Interviews.
- Observation.
- Usage data.
- Feedback.
- Experiments.

## Product Decisions

Prioritize:

- Customer impact.
- Learning value.
- Strategic importance.
- Simplicity.

## Feature Decisions

A feature should answer:

- Which problem does it solve?
- For whom?
- How often?
- What value does it create?

## Product Quality

Consider:

- Usability.
- Reliability.
- Performance.
- Trust.
- Experience.

## Common Mistakes

- Building features without problems.
- Listening only to loud users.
- Confusing complexity with value.
- Ignoring onboarding.
- Optimizing short-term metrics.

## Founder Academy Standard

Founders should think from customer outcomes backward, not from features forward.
