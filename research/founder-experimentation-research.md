# Founder Experimentation — Research Report

## Purpose

Founder experimentation defines how founders test assumptions, reduce uncertainty, and discover effective solutions through structured learning.

The objective is replacing opinions with evidence.

## Core Principles

Every experiment should:

- Test an important assumption.
- Be measurable.
- Be small enough to run quickly.
- Produce a clear learning outcome.

## Experiment Structure

## Hypothesis

Define:

- What we believe.
- Why we believe it.
- What should happen if it is true.

Example:

"If customers understand this value proposition, conversion will increase."

## Experiment

Choose the smallest possible test.

Examples:

- Customer interview.
- Landing page.
- Prototype.
- Manual service.
- Pricing test.
- Feature test.

## Measurement

Define:

- Success metric.
- Failure metric.
- Time period.
- Decision rule.

## Learning

After the experiment:

Ask:

- What happened?
- Why?
- What changed?
- What should we test next?

## Experiment Prioritization

Consider:

- Impact.
- Confidence.
- Effort.

Prioritize experiments that can create the most learning with the least cost.

## Common Mistakes

- Building before testing.
- Running experiments without hypotheses.
- Changing many variables.
- Ignoring negative results.
- Testing low-impact assumptions.

## Founder Academy Standard

The founder's advantage is learning faster than competitors.
