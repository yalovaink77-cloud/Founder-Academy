# Value Proposition — Research Report

## Purpose

A value proposition explains why a customer should choose this product instead of continuing with the current solution.

The objective is clarity.

## Core Principles

A strong value proposition explains:

- Who the customer is.
- Which problem is solved.
- Which outcome is delivered.
- Why this solution is better.
- Why it is believable.

## Components

### Customer

Who benefits?

### Problem

Which important problem exists?

### Solution

How is the problem solved?

### Outcome

What measurable improvement occurs?

### Differentiation

Why is this better than alternatives?

### Proof

Why should the customer trust this claim?

## Evaluation

A value proposition should be:

- Clear
- Relevant
- Credible
- Specific
- Measurable
- Customer-focused

## Common Mistakes

- Talking about features instead of outcomes.
- Using technical language.
- Trying to serve everyone.
- Making unsupported claims.
- Ignoring competitors.

## Validation

Validate using:

- Interviews
- Landing pages
- A/B tests
- Customer behaviour
- Conversion
- Retention

## Founder Academy Standard

Customers buy outcomes, not product descriptions.
