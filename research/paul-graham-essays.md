# Paul Graham — Essays Research

## Core Philosophy

Startups succeed by solving real problems for real people.

Technology alone is not a competitive advantage.

Understanding users is.

## Start Small

Successful startups often begin by serving a very small market exceptionally well.

Expansion comes after deep customer understanding.

## Build For Users

Founders should obsess over users rather than competitors.

Questions:

- What frustrates users?
- What delights users?
- What saves them time?
- What creates trust?

## Make Something People Want

The product should solve an important problem.

People should naturally recommend it.

If customers are indifferent, the product is not ready.

## Founder Advantage

Early-stage founders possess unique advantages:

- Speed
- Direct customer access
- Rapid decision making
- Flexibility

Large companies usually cannot move as quickly.

## Growth

Early growth is usually slow.

Slow early growth does not necessarily indicate failure.

Improvement should focus on:

- Product quality
- User happiness
- Retention
- Word of mouth

## Competition

Competitors matter less than customer value.

Better customer understanding is a stronger advantage than feature parity.

## Default Alive

A startup should preserve enough resources to continue learning.

Revenue, expenses, and growth should be monitored continuously.

## Product Decisions

Prioritize:

- Important problems
- Simplicity
- Customer feedback
- Fast iteration

Avoid unnecessary complexity.

## Founder Academy Standard

Founders should optimize for customer understanding before optimization of scale.

Small groups of delighted users are more valuable than large groups of indifferent users.

## What Founder Academy Accepts

- Start with a small market
- Obsess over users
- Solve meaningful problems
- Iterate rapidly
- Preserve flexibility
- Focus on retention before scale
- Default Alive thinking

## What Founder Academy Rejects

- Building for investors instead of users
- Feature accumulation without customer value
- Scaling before customer satisfaction
- Chasing competitors instead of customers
- Measuring success only by publicity

## AI Era Relevance

AI dramatically reduces product development cost.

Customer understanding, trust, distribution, and execution remain competitive advantages.

## Actionable Workflow

1. Choose a narrow customer.
2. Understand the problem deeply.
3. Build the smallest valuable solution.
4. Talk with users continuously.
5. Improve rapidly.
6. Create delighted users.
7. Expand gradually.
8. Preserve financial runway.

## Founder Academy Principle

Better customer understanding beats better technology.
