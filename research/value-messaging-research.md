# Value Messaging — Research Report

## Purpose

Value messaging communicates product value clearly enough that the right customer immediately understands why the product matters.

Messaging should communicate outcomes rather than features.

## Core Principles

Customers care about:

- Better outcomes
- Faster results
- Lower cost
- Lower risk
- Simplicity
- Confidence

They rarely care about implementation details.

## Messaging Hierarchy

1. Problem
2. Outcome
3. Solution
4. Differentiation
5. Proof
6. Call To Action

## Message Evaluation

Every message should answer:

- Who is this for?
- Which problem is solved?
- Why now?
- Why this product?
- Why trust it?
- What should happen next?

## Message Qualities

A strong message is:

- Clear
- Specific
- Customer-focused
- Credible
- Outcome-driven
- Easy to remember

## Validation

Validate messaging using:

- Landing pages
- A/B tests
- Customer interviews
- Sales calls
- Conversion rates
- Activation

## Common Mistakes

- Talking about features.
- Using technical language.
- Multiple primary messages.
- Unsupported claims.
- Weak calls to action.

## Founder Academy Standard

Good messaging reduces customer confusion and increases conversion.
