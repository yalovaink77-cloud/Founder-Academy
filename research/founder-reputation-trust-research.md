# Founder Reputation & Trust — Research Report

## Purpose

Founder reputation and trust define how customers, employees, investors, and communities perceive the founder and the company.

The objective is building credibility that compounds over time.

## Core Principles

Trust is created through:

- Consistent actions.
- Honest communication.
- Delivering promises.
- Transparency.
- Responsible decisions.

## Reputation Sources

## Customer Trust

Built through:

- Product quality.
- Reliability.
- Customer support.
- Honest marketing.

## Team Trust

Built through:

- Fair leadership.
- Clear expectations.
- Keeping commitments.
- Respect.

## Investor Trust

Built through:

- Accurate reporting.
- Transparency.
- Responsible resource management.

## Community Trust

Built through:

- Sharing knowledge.
- Contributing value.
- Respecting relationships.

## Reputation Compounds

Positive reputation creates:

- Easier recruiting.
- Stronger partnerships.
- Customer confidence.
- Better opportunities.

Negative reputation creates:

- Higher friction.
- Lower trust.
- Long-term damage.

## Trust Recovery

When mistakes happen:

1. Acknowledge the problem.
2. Communicate clearly.
3. Fix the issue.
4. Learn publicly.
5. Prevent repetition.

## Common Mistakes

- Overpromising.
- Hiding problems.
- Ignoring feedback.
- Sacrificing trust for short-term growth.

## Founder Academy Standard

Reputation is a strategic asset built through repeated trustworthy actions.
