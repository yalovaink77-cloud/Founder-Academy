# Founder Innovation — Research Report

## Purpose

Founder innovation describes how entrepreneurs discover new opportunities, create new solutions, and improve existing systems.

The objective is creating meaningful value through better approaches.

## Core Principles

Innovation is not only invention.

Innovation combines:

- Customer problems.
- New insights.
- Better solutions.
- Effective execution.

## Types Of Innovation

## Product Innovation

Improving or creating products that solve customer problems.

Examples:

- New capabilities.
- Better experiences.
- Improved outcomes.

## Process Innovation

Improving how work is performed.

Examples:

- Faster operations.
- Lower costs.
- Better quality.

## Business Model Innovation

Changing how value is created and captured.

Examples:

- New pricing models.
- New distribution models.
- New customer relationships.

## Market Innovation

Creating or entering new markets.

Examples:

- New customer segments.
- New use cases.

## Innovation Process

1. Identify problems.
2. Generate ideas.
3. Test assumptions.
4. Build experiments.
5. Measure results.
6. Improve.

## Customer-Driven Innovation

Strong innovation starts with:

- Customer observation.
- Unmet needs.
- Frustrations.
- Behaviour patterns.

## Common Mistakes

- Building technology without demand.
- Confusing novelty with value.
- Ignoring customers.
- Innovating without execution.

## Founder Academy Standard

Innovation creates value when a new approach solves an important problem better than existing alternatives.
