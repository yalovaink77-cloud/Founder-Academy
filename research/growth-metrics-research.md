# Growth Metrics — Research Report

## Purpose

Growth metrics measure whether a company is acquiring, activating, retaining, and expanding customers sustainably.

The objective is understanding growth quality, not only growth speed.

## Core Principles

A good growth metric:

- Measures meaningful customer behaviour.
- Supports decisions.
- Connects to business outcomes.
- Can be improved through action.

## Growth Funnel Metrics

## Acquisition

Measures customer discovery.

Examples:

- Visitors.
- Leads.
- Signups.
- Cost per acquisition.

Questions:

- Which channels work?
- Which customers arrive?

## Activation

Measures first value.

Examples:

- Completed onboarding.
- First successful action.
- Time to value.

Questions:

- Do users understand the product?
- Do they reach value quickly?

## Retention

Measures ongoing value.

Examples:

- Returning users.
- Active users.
- Churn.

Questions:

- Why do users stay?
- Why do users leave?

## Revenue

Measures business sustainability.

Examples:

- MRR.
- ARR.
- ARPU.
- LTV.

Questions:

- Are customers creating economic value?

## Referral

Measures customer-driven growth.

Examples:

- Invitations.
- Recommendations.
- Sharing.

## North Star Metric

A North Star Metric represents the core value delivered to customers.

A good North Star Metric:

- Reflects customer value.
- Predicts business health.
- Guides decisions.

## Common Mistakes

- Optimizing only acquisition.
- Ignoring retention.
- Measuring activity instead of value.
- Using vanity metrics.

## Founder Academy Standard

Growth metrics should explain whether the company is creating durable customer value.
