# Pricing Validation — Research Report

## Purpose

Pricing validation determines whether customers are willing to pay the proposed price under real market conditions.

The objective is to validate willingness to pay before scaling.

## Core Principles

Customers often overstate willingness to pay.

Real purchasing behaviour is stronger than verbal agreement.

## Validation Methods

- Paid pilot
- Pre-order
- Stripe payment page
- Pricing page A/B test
- Sales conversations
- Enterprise proposals
- Limited-time offers

## Strong Evidence

- Completed payment
- Signed contract
- Paid pilot
- Annual subscription
- Upgrade
- Expansion

## Weak Evidence

- "I'd pay."
- Survey responses
- Likes
- Downloads
- Waiting list

## Metrics

Track:

- Conversion
- Average Revenue Per User
- Lifetime Value
- Churn
- Expansion Revenue
- Refund Rate

## Common Mistakes

- Asking hypothetical pricing questions.
- Never increasing prices.
- Competing only on price.
- Confusing interest with payment.

## Founder Academy Standard

Pricing is validated only when customers repeatedly exchange money for value.
