# Launch Playbook — Research Report

## Purpose

A launch playbook converts launch strategy into repeatable execution steps.

The objective is coordinated market entry, measurable learning, and controlled improvement.

## Core Principles

A launch requires alignment between:

- Product
- Customer
- Positioning
- Distribution
- Measurement
- Support

## Launch Preparation

Confirm:

- Target customer.
- Problem.
- Value proposition.
- Positioning.
- Pricing.
- Distribution channels.
- Analytics.
- Support process.

## Launch Phases

### Pre-Launch

Goals:

- Build awareness.
- Recruit early users.
- Prepare communication.
- Test messaging.

### Launch

Goals:

- Acquire users.
- Measure behaviour.
- Collect feedback.
- Identify issues.

### Post-Launch

Goals:

- Improve retention.
- Fix problems.
- Optimize conversion.
- Improve economics.

## Communication

Prepare:

- Website.
- Documentation.
- Emails.
- Social content.
- Support responses.

## Measurement

Track:

- Visitors.
- Signups.
- Activation.
- Retention.
- Conversion.
- Revenue.

## Common Mistakes

- No launch objective.
- No measurement.
- No support plan.
- Focusing only on launch day.
- Ignoring post-launch learning.

## Founder Academy Standard

A launch playbook is a repeatable learning system, not a one-time announcement.
