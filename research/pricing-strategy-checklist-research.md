# Pricing Strategy Checklist — Research Report

## Purpose

A pricing checklist ensures pricing decisions are based on customer value, evidence, and business sustainability.

## Customer Understanding

Confirm:

- Target customer is defined.
- Customer problem is understood.
- Customer value is measurable.
- Alternatives are known.
- Willingness to pay is investigated.

## Value Assessment

Evaluate:

- Time saved.
- Money saved.
- Revenue created.
- Risk reduced.
- Convenience improved.
- Emotional value.

## Pricing Structure

Review:

- Pricing model.
- Number of plans.
- Feature boundaries.
- Upgrade paths.
- Discount strategy.
- Enterprise approach.

## Validation

Confirm:

- Real customers have paid.
- Pricing has been tested.
- Conversion is measured.
- Retention is tracked.
- Churn is understood.

## Business Health

Measure:

- Revenue.
- ARPU.
- LTV.
- CAC.
- Gross margin.
- Expansion revenue.

## Common Mistakes

- Pricing from cost.
- Copying competitors.
- Avoiding price testing.
- Making pricing too complex.
- Ignoring customer value.

## Founder Academy Standard

A pricing strategy should be continuously tested and improved as customer understanding increases.
