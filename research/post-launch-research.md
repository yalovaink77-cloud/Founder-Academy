# Post-Launch Strategy — Research Report

## Purpose

Post-launch strategy defines how a product learns, improves, and grows after release.

Launch is the beginning of continuous iteration.

## Core Principles

After launch, the company should focus on:

- Customer behaviour.
- Retention.
- Product improvement.
- Revenue.
- Distribution learning.

## First Priorities

After launch:

1. Monitor users.
2. Identify problems.
3. Fix critical issues.
4. Improve onboarding.
5. Measure retention.
6. Improve conversion.

## Metrics

Track:

### Acquisition

How users discover the product.

### Activation

Whether users experience first value.

### Retention

Whether users continue using the product.

### Revenue

Whether customers create sustainable business value.

### Referral

Whether users recommend the product.

## Customer Feedback

Collect:

- Interviews.
- Support requests.
- Reviews.
- Analytics.
- Behaviour patterns.

Prioritize based on impact.

## Growth Decisions

Evaluate:

- What works?
- What fails?
- Which customers succeed?
- Which channels perform?
- Which improvements matter?

## Common Mistakes

- Celebrating launch instead of measuring outcomes.
- Ignoring churn.
- Adding features without evidence.
- Scaling acquisition before retention.

## Founder Academy Standard

Post-launch is a continuous validation process.

The product should improve based on evidence, not assumptions.
