# Founder Story — Research Report

## Purpose

A founder story explains why the company exists, why the founder cares, and why customers should trust the mission.

The objective is creating meaning, trust, and connection.

## Core Principles

A strong founder story connects:

- Personal experience.
- Customer problem.
- Company mission.
- Future vision.

## Components

## Origin

Questions:

- Why did this company start?
- What was personally observed?
- What frustration existed?

## Problem

Explain:

- Who experiences the problem?
- Why does it matter?
- Why is it worth solving?

## Insight

Describe:

- What was discovered?
- Why existing solutions were insufficient?

## Mission

Define:

- What change the company wants to create.
- Who benefits.

## Credibility

Trust comes from:

- Experience.
- Learning.
- Evidence.
- Customer understanding.

## Common Mistakes

- Making the founder the hero instead of the customer.
- Creating artificial stories.
- Exaggerating achievements.
- Focusing on features instead of purpose.

## Founder Academy Standard

A founder story should explain the connection between founder motivation and customer value.
