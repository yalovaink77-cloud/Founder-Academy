# Distribution Checklist — Research Report

## Purpose

A distribution checklist ensures that customer acquisition decisions are based on evidence, economics, and repeatability.

## Customer

Confirm:

- Target customer defined.
- Customer problem understood.
- Customer location and behaviour known.
- Customer discovery completed.

## Channel

Evaluate:

- Channel fit.
- Customer reachability.
- Acquisition cost.
- Scalability.
- Competition.

## Message

Confirm:

- Clear value proposition.
- Customer language used.
- Differentiation explained.
- Proof available.

## Conversion

Measure:

- Landing page conversion.
- Signup conversion.
- Activation.
- Sales conversion.
- Purchase conversion.

## Economics

Track:

- Customer Acquisition Cost.
- Lifetime Value.
- Payback period.
- Revenue.
- Retention.

## Testing

Every channel requires:

- Hypothesis.
- Experiment.
- Metric.
- Time limit.
- Decision.

## Common Mistakes

- Scaling before validation.
- Optimizing traffic only.
- Ignoring retention.
- Choosing channels because competitors use them.

## Founder Academy Standard

A distribution channel is valuable only when it can repeatedly acquire valuable customers.
