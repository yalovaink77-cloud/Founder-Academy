# Growth Loops — Research Report

## Purpose

Growth loops describe systems where existing user activity creates additional growth.

The objective is building repeatable growth mechanisms rather than relying only on campaigns.

## Core Principles

Traditional funnel:

Traffic → Conversion → Customer

Growth loop:

Input → Action → Value → New Input

A successful loop compounds over time.

## Types Of Growth Loops

## Viral Loop

Existing users bring new users.

Examples:

- Invitations.
- Sharing.
- Collaboration.

Requires:

- User motivation.
- Easy sharing.
- Recipient value.

## Content Loop

Users or company create content that attracts new users.

Examples:

- SEO.
- Community content.
- Educational resources.

## Referral Loop

Satisfied customers recommend the product.

Requires:

- Customer satisfaction.
- Trust.
- Simple referral process.

## Product Loop

Product usage naturally creates growth.

Examples:

- Collaboration.
- Shared documents.
- Network effects.

## Marketplace Loop

More supply creates more demand and more demand creates more supply.

## Measuring Growth Loops

Track:

- Loop input.
- Conversion.
- Activation.
- Retention.
- Referral.
- Growth rate.

## Common Mistakes

- Copying viral tactics without value.
- Optimizing acquisition before retention.
- Ignoring product quality.
- Treating campaigns as loops.

## Founder Academy Standard

The strongest growth comes from systems where customer success creates future growth.
