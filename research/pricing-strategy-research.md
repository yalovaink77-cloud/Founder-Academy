# Pricing Strategy — Research Report

## Purpose

Pricing determines how the business captures value.

Customers pay for outcomes, not features.

## Core Principles

Good pricing reflects:

- Customer value
- Willingness to pay
- Market positioning
- Business sustainability

Pricing is a business decision, not an engineering decision.

## Pricing Models

- Subscription
- One-time purchase
- Usage-based
- Tiered pricing
- Freemium
- Enterprise
- Marketplace commission
- Hybrid

## Pricing Factors

Evaluate:

- Customer value
- Alternatives
- Switching cost
- Frequency of use
- Cost savings
- Revenue impact
- Time savings
- Competitive positioning

## Validation

Validate pricing using:

- Customer interviews
- Paid pilots
- Pre-orders
- A/B pricing tests
- Conversion
- Retention
- Revenue

## Common Mistakes

- Pricing based on development cost.
- Competing only on price.
- Never testing prices.
- Charging too little.
- Ignoring willingness to pay.

## Founder Academy Standard

Pricing should maximize long-term customer value and business sustainability.
