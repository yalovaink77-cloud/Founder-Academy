# Founder Responsibility — Research Report

## Purpose

Founder responsibility defines the obligations founders have toward customers, teams, investors, communities, and the broader impact of the company.

The objective is building companies that create value responsibly.

## Core Principles

Founders are responsible for:

- Decisions.
- Consequences.
- Trust.
- Long-term impact.

Responsibility increases as company influence grows.

## Customer Responsibility

Founders should:

- Solve meaningful problems.
- Protect user interests.
- Communicate honestly.
- Respect privacy.
- Deliver promised value.

## Team Responsibility

Founders should:

- Create fair environments.
- Provide clarity.
- Develop people.
- Build trust.

## Business Responsibility

Founders should:

- Manage resources carefully.
- Understand risks.
- Make sustainable decisions.
- Avoid harmful shortcuts.

## Product Responsibility

Consider:

- Security.
- Privacy.
- Reliability.
- Accessibility.
- Possible unintended effects.

## Decision Responsibility

Good decisions require:

- Evidence.
- Awareness of consequences.
- Long-term thinking.
- Accountability.

## Common Mistakes

- Optimizing only growth.
- Ignoring consequences.
- Avoiding responsibility during problems.
- Sacrificing trust for short-term results.

## Founder Academy Standard

A founder is responsible not only for what the company achieves, but also how it achieves it.
