# Beta Tester Recruitment — Research Report

## Purpose

Beta tester recruitment identifies users who can provide meaningful product feedback before wider release.

The objective is not maximum tester count.

The objective is high-quality learning.

## Core Principles

Good testers:

- Match the target customer.
- Experience the problem.
- Use the product realistically.
- Provide honest feedback.
- Represent future users.

## Recruitment Sources

### Existing Network

Useful for:

- First feedback.
- Early trust.
- Fast learning.

Risk:

May not represent real customers.

### Communities

Examples:

- Industry groups.
- Online communities.
- Forums.
- Professional networks.

Benefit:

Access to relevant users.

### Customer Discovery

Users already interviewed may become beta testers.

Benefit:

Existing context and trust.

### Waitlists

Useful when:

- Audience is already qualified.
- Problem awareness exists.

## Tester Selection

Evaluate:

- Problem relevance.
- Motivation.
- Availability.
- Feedback quality.
- Usage likelihood.

## Beta Size

Start small.

Small groups provide:

- Better communication.
- Faster learning.
- Easier problem identification.

Increase size after major risks decrease.

## Common Mistakes

- Recruiting random users.
- Choosing only friends.
- Optimizing tester quantity.
- Ignoring inactive testers.
- No onboarding process.

## Founder Academy Standard

The best beta testers are future customers, not random users.
