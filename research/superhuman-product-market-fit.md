# Superhuman — Product-Market Fit Case Study

## Background

Superhuman focused on achieving Product-Market Fit before aggressive growth.

The company treated Product-Market Fit as a measurable objective rather than a feeling.

## Initial Assumption

Professionals would pay a premium for a dramatically faster email experience.

## Validation Method

- Founder interviews
- Continuous customer interviews
- Usage analysis
- Retention analysis
- Product iteration

## Product-Market Fit Framework

Customers were asked:

"How would you feel if you could no longer use Superhuman?"

Possible answers:

- Very disappointed
- Somewhat disappointed
- Not disappointed

A Product-Market Fit signal was reached when approximately 40% of surveyed users answered:

Very disappointed.

## Segmentation

The survey results were segmented by:

- Customer type
- Job role
- Usage frequency
- Customer behaviour

The product was improved primarily for users in the "Very disappointed" segment.

## Continuous Improvement

The team repeatedly asked:

- What do these users love?
- What do they dislike?
- What alternatives do they use?
- What nearly prevented adoption?

The roadmap focused on increasing the percentage of highly satisfied users.

## What Worked

- Continuous interviews
- Data-driven prioritization
- Customer segmentation
- Retention focus
- Deliberate product improvement

## What Failed

Product-Market Fit was not assumed after launch.

It required repeated measurement.

## Founder Academy Standard

Product-Market Fit should be measured continuously.

Retention and customer disappointment are stronger indicators than downloads or publicity.

## Actionable Rules

- Measure customer disappointment.
- Segment customers.
- Improve the happiest users first.
- Track retention continuously.
- Delay aggressive growth until Product-Market Fit improves.

## What Founder Academy Accepts

- Product-Market Fit surveys
- Customer segmentation
- Continuous interviews
- Retention-first thinking
- Data-driven prioritization

## What Founder Academy Rejects

- Assuming Product-Market Fit after launch.
- Using downloads as proof of success.
- Scaling acquisition before retention.

## AI Era Relevance

AI accelerates product development.

Product-Market Fit still depends on customer behaviour, retention, and willingness to continue using the product.

## Founder Academy Principle

Growth should amplify Product-Market Fit, not compensate for its absence.
