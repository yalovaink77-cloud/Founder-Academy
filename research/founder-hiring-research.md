# Founder Hiring — Research Report

## Purpose

Founder hiring defines how startups build teams that increase execution capability without creating unnecessary complexity.

The objective is hiring the right people at the right stage.

## Core Principles

Early hiring should solve critical constraints.

A new hire should:

- Increase company capability.
- Own an important problem.
- Improve speed or quality.
- Fit company values.

## Hiring Before Product-Market Fit

Before repeatability:

Prioritize:

- Founders.
- Small expert teams.
- Flexible contributors.

Avoid:

- Large teams.
- Complex management structures.
- Hiring for status.

## Hiring Criteria

Evaluate:

### Ability

Can this person solve the problem?

### Ownership

Will they take responsibility?

### Learning Speed

Can they adapt under uncertainty?

### Alignment

Do they understand the mission and standards?

### Communication

Can they work effectively with others?

## First Employees

Early team members should usually be:

- High ownership.
- Comfortable with ambiguity.
- Generalists with strong skills.
- Customer-focused.

## Hiring Process

Steps:

1. Define the problem.
2. Define success.
3. Identify required skills.
4. Evaluate evidence.
5. Test collaboration.
6. Make decision.

## Common Mistakes

- Hiring too early.
- Hiring impressive resumes without fit.
- Ignoring culture.
- Hiring because of workload instead of strategic need.
- Keeping weak performers too long.

## Founder Academy Standard

Hire when a person creates more value than the complexity they introduce.
