# First 100 Users — Research Report

## Purpose

The first users provide the earliest evidence that a product solves a real problem.

The objective is learning, not rapid scale.

## Core Principles

Early customers are usually found through:

- Direct outreach
- Personal networks
- Communities
- Founder conversations
- Manual sales
- Existing relationships

## Founder Role

Early founders should personally handle:

- Customer discovery
- Sales
- Onboarding
- Support
- Feedback collection

This creates deeper understanding.

## Acquisition Methods

### Direct Outreach

Useful for:

- B2B
- Niche products
- Early validation

### Communities

Examples:

- Forums
- Reddit
- Professional groups
- Industry communities

Value must come before promotion.

### Content

Useful for:

- Education
- Trust building
- Long-term acquisition

### Partnerships

Useful when another organization already reaches the target customer.

## First User Criteria

Good early users:

- Have the problem.
- Feel the pain.
- Are willing to try early products.
- Give detailed feedback.
- May recommend the product.

## Common Mistakes

- Waiting for customers to discover the product.
- Advertising before understanding users.
- Ignoring feedback.
- Choosing users only because they are easy to reach.
- Optimizing quantity instead of learning.

## Measurement

Track:

- Activation
- Retention
- Feedback quality
- Referrals
- Conversion
- Revenue

## Founder Academy Standard

The first 100 users are not just customers.

They are the foundation of product understanding.
