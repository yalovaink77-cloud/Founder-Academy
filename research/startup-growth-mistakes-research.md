# Startup Growth Mistakes — Research Report

## Purpose

Understanding growth mistakes helps founders avoid scaling problems before the business model is ready.

Growth should amplify a working system.

It cannot fix fundamental problems.

## Core Growth Mistakes

## 1. Scaling Before Product-Market Fit

Problem:

Companies invest heavily in acquisition before customers receive enough value.

Signals:

- High churn.
- Low retention.
- Expensive acquisition.
- Weak referrals.

Solution:

Improve:

- Product value.
- Activation.
- Retention.

Before scaling.

## 2. Chasing Vanity Metrics

Problem:

Numbers increase without creating business value.

Examples:

- Downloads.
- Followers.
- Page views.
- Impressions.

Better metrics:

- Retention.
- Revenue.
- Activation.
- Referral.

## 3. Ignoring Retention

Problem:

Acquiring customers who leave quickly creates an unsustainable business.

Focus:

- First value.
- Product experience.
- Customer success.

## 4. Too Many Growth Channels

Problem:

Testing every channel prevents learning.

Better approach:

- Select promising channels.
- Run focused experiments.
- Measure results.

## 5. Growth Without Economics

Problem:

Revenue growth can hide an unhealthy business.

Monitor:

- CAC.
- LTV.
- Margin.
- Payback period.

## 6. Copying Competitors

Problem:

Competitor tactics may not fit your customers or market.

Focus:

- Customer problems.
- Unique advantages.
- Evidence.

## 7. Hiring Too Early

Problem:

More people do not automatically create growth.

Early priority:

- Learning.
- Customer understanding.
- Repeatability.

## Founder Academy Standard

Growth is a consequence of customer value, not a substitute for it.
