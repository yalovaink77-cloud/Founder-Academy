# Founder Vision — Research Report

## Purpose

Founder vision defines the future state a company aims to create.

The objective is providing long-term direction while allowing strategy to evolve.

## Core Principles

A strong vision:

- Describes a meaningful future.
- Creates alignment.
- Guides difficult decisions.
- Inspires long-term commitment.

## Vision Components

## Future State

What should exist in the future?

## Customer Impact

How will customers be better?

## Market Change

What transformation should happen?

## Company Role

What unique contribution will the company make?

## Vision vs Strategy

Vision:

- Long-term destination.

Strategy:

- Current path to reach that destination.

Strategy changes.

Vision may remain stable.

## Benefits

A clear vision helps:

- Prioritize opportunities.
- Reject distractions.
- Align teams.
- Communicate purpose.

## Common Mistakes

- Confusing vision with goals.
- Making unrealistic statements.
- Creating vague inspirational phrases.
- Ignoring customer impact.

## Founder Academy Standard

Vision provides direction, while evidence determines the path.
