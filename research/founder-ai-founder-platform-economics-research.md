# Founder AI Founder Platform Economics — Research Report

## Purpose

Founder AI founder platform economics describes how founders use artificial intelligence, network effects, business model design, and economic analysis to understand how platforms create, capture, and sustain value.

The objective is building platforms where increasing participation creates stronger economic advantages over time.

## Core Principles

A strong platform economics system requires:

- Clear value exchange.
- Sustainable incentives.
- Efficient transactions.
- Network effects.
- Scalable infrastructure.

Platforms succeed when every additional participant increases value for other participants.
