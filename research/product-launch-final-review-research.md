# Product Launch Final Review — Research Report

## Purpose

Final launch review evaluates whether the product, market, operations, and measurement systems are ready for broader release.

The objective is reducing avoidable launch risk.

## Product Readiness

Confirm:

- Core user problem validated.
- Main user workflow works.
- Critical bugs resolved.
- Performance acceptable.
- Support process ready.

## Customer Readiness

Confirm:

- Target customer defined.
- Customer language understood.
- Value proposition clear.
- Positioning tested.

## Business Readiness

Confirm:

- Pricing ready.
- Revenue model understood.
- Customer acquisition path tested.
- Economics measured.

## Distribution Readiness

Confirm:

- Channels selected.
- Messaging prepared.
- Tracking installed.
- Acquisition process ready.

## Measurement Readiness

Track:

- Acquisition.
- Activation.
- Retention.
- Conversion.
- Revenue.
- Feedback.

## Risk Review

Identify:

- Technical risks.
- Customer risks.
- Market risks.
- Operational risks.
- Financial risks.

## Launch Decision

Possible outcomes:

### Proceed

Evidence supports release.

### Delay

Important risks remain.

### Modify

Adjust product, positioning, or strategy.

### Stop

Evidence indicates the opportunity should not continue.

## Founder Academy Standard

A launch decision should be based on evidence, not excitement or deadlines.
