# Customer Profile — Research Report

## Purpose

A customer profile defines the initial customer with enough precision to support validation, positioning, product decisions, pricing, and distribution.

The objective is operational clarity rather than demographic description.

## Core Principles

A customer profile should describe:

- Who they are.
- What they are trying to achieve.
- Which problem they experience.
- Why the problem matters.
- How they solve it today.
- Why current solutions are insufficient.
- What would motivate switching.

## Required Components

### Customer

- Role
- Industry
- Experience
- Environment

### Goals

- Primary objective
- Success criteria

### Problems

- Frequency
- Severity
- Consequences

### Current Alternatives

- Existing products
- Manual work
- Internal solutions
- Competitors

### Buying

- Buyer
- User
- Decision maker
- Budget owner

### Constraints

- Budget
- Time
- Technology
- Risk tolerance

## Validation

Profiles should be based on interviews and behavioural evidence.

Profiles should evolve as new evidence appears.

## Common Mistakes

- Targeting everyone.
- Using only demographics.
- Ignoring buying authority.
- Assuming motivations.
- Never updating profiles.

## Founder Academy Standard

Customer profiles are living research documents built from evidence rather than assumptions.
