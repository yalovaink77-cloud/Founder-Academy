# Growth Experiments — Research Report

## Purpose

Growth experiments are controlled tests designed to discover which actions improve customer acquisition, activation, retention, or revenue.

The objective is learning, not random optimization.

## Core Principles

Every experiment should define:

- Hypothesis.
- Target audience.
- Expected behaviour.
- Metric.
- Success threshold.
- Failure threshold.
- Decision.

## Experiment Framework

### Problem

What opportunity or weakness are we testing?

### Hypothesis

What do we believe will happen?

Example:

"If we improve onboarding, more users will reach first value."

### Experiment

What is the smallest test?

Examples:

- Landing page change.
- Pricing test.
- Email sequence.
- Feature change.
- Channel test.

### Measurement

What result determines success?

### Decision

Possible outcomes:

- Continue.
- Expand.
- Modify.
- Stop.

## Experiment Prioritization

Evaluate:

- Impact.
- Confidence.
- Effort.

High-impact, high-confidence, low-effort experiments should be tested first.

## Common Mistakes

- Running experiments without hypotheses.
- Changing multiple variables.
- Ignoring negative results.
- Optimizing before understanding users.
- Measuring vanity metrics.

## Founder Academy Standard

Every experiment must create a clear learning outcome.
