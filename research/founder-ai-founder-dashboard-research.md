# Founder AI Founder Dashboard — Research Report

## Purpose

Founder AI founder dashboards describe systems where founders use artificial intelligence, analytics, and connected information sources to maintain a clear view of company performance, priorities, risks, and opportunities.

The objective is creating a decision center that helps founders focus on what matters most.

## Core Principles

A founder dashboard requires:

- Relevant information.
- Clear metrics.
- Real-time awareness.
- Actionable insights.
- Decision support.

A dashboard should reduce uncertainty, not create more information overload.

## Dashboard Layers

## Company Health Layer

Tracks:

- Revenue.
- Costs.
- Growth.
- Key business metrics.

Purpose:

Understand overall company condition.

## Customer Layer

Tracks:

- Acquisition.
- Engagement.
- Retention.
- Feedback.

Purpose:

Understand customer reality.

## Product Layer

Tracks:

- Usage.
- Features.
- Experiments.
- Issues.

Purpose:

Understand product performance.

## Operations Layer

Tracks:

- Processes.
- Tasks.
- Bottlenecks.
- Efficiency.

Purpose:

Understand execution.

## Strategic Layer

Tracks:

- Goals.
- Priorities.
- Risks.
- Opportunities.

Purpose:

Maintain strategic focus.

## AI Role

AI can support:

- Summaries.
- Trend detection.
- Anomaly identification.
- Recommendations.
- Decision preparation.

## Founder Responsibility

Founder owns:

- What matters.
- Interpretation.
- Priorities.
- Actions.

## Benefits

AI founder dashboards can create:

- Faster awareness.
- Better decisions.
- Reduced information overload.
- Stronger execution.

## Common Mistakes

- Tracking too many metrics.
- Confusing activity with progress.
- Ignoring qualitative information.
- Building dashboards nobody uses.

## Founder Academy Standard

The best founder dashboard answers one question:

"What requires my attention now?"
