# Founder Responsibility

## Purpose

Build companies responsibly while creating lasting value.

## Responsibilities

### Customers

- Solve real problems.
- Protect trust.
- Respect privacy.

### Team

- Create fairness.
- Provide clarity.
- Build trust.

### Business

- Manage resources.
- Understand risks.
- Think long term.

### Product

- Consider security.
- Consider impact.
- Consider consequences.

## Founder Academy Standard

Success includes both what is built and how it is built.

## Research Basis

- YC
- High Output Management
- Responsible Innovation Research
