# Founder Product Thinking

## Purpose

Build products based on customer value and real problems.

## Principles

Start with:

- Problem.
- Customer.
- Outcome.

Not:

- Features.
- Technology.
- Assumptions.

## Product Decisions

Evaluate:

- Customer impact.
- Learning.
- Strategic value.
- Simplicity.

## Avoid

- Feature obsession.
- Building without validation.
- Ignoring user behaviour.

## Founder Academy Standard

Great products solve important problems simply.
