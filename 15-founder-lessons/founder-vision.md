# Founder Vision

## Purpose

Define the future state the company wants to create.

## Components

Future State

↓

Customer Impact

↓

Market Change

↓

Company Role

## Vision

Explains:

- Where we are going.
- Why it matters.

## Strategy

Explains:

- How we get there.

## Principles

- Vision stays stable.
- Strategy adapts.
- Evidence guides decisions.

## Founder Academy Standard

A strong vision creates direction without preventing adaptation.
