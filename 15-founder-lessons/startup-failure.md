# Startup Failure

## Purpose

Understand common reasons startups fail and reduce avoidable mistakes.

## Major Risks

- No customer need.
- Running out of money.
- Weak Product-Market Fit.
- Poor execution.
- Wrong timing.
- Ignoring alternatives.

## Prevention

- Validate early.
- Measure behaviour.
- Preserve cash.
- Talk with customers.
- Experiment continuously.

## Founder Academy Standard

Evidence reduces risk.
