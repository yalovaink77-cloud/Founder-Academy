# Founder AI-First Company

## Purpose

Build companies where AI is a core operating capability.

## Characteristics

- AI-enabled workflows.
- Knowledge systems.
- Automation.
- Data-driven decisions.

## Founder Role

Own:

- Vision.
- Strategy.
- Customer value.
- Responsibility.

## Advantages

- Speed.
- Efficiency.
- Learning.
- Personalization.

## Avoid

- AI without purpose.
- Automation without understanding.
- Ignoring fundamentals.

## Founder Academy Standard

AI-first means redesigning how the company operates, not simply adding AI features.

## Research Basis

- OpenAI Research
- YC
- Lean Startup
