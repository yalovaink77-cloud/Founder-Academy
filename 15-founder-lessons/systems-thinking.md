# Founder Systems Thinking

## Purpose

Understand the company as an interconnected system.

## Systems Include

- Customers.
- Product.
- Growth.
- Revenue.
- Operations.

## Principles

Look for:

- Relationships.
- Feedback loops.
- Root causes.
- Long-term effects.

## Avoid

- Symptom fixing.
- Isolated decisions.
- Short-term optimization.

## Founder Academy Standard

Founders should build systems that continuously improve.

## Research Basis

- Thinking in Systems
- High Output Management
- YC
