# Founder Simplicity

## Purpose

Reduce complexity to improve focus and execution.

## Principles

Focus on:

- Important problems.
- Clear priorities.
- Customer value.
- Simple systems.

## Simplify

- Product.
- Strategy.
- Communication.
- Operations.

## Avoid

- Feature overload.
- Too many priorities.
- Unnecessary processes.

## Founder Academy Standard

Simple systems create faster learning and better decisions.

## Research Basis

- YC
- Good Strategy Bad Strategy
- Essentialism
