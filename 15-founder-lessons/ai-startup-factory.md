# Founder AI Startup Factory

## Purpose

Create a repeatable system for discovering and building businesses.

## Components

- Research engine.
- Validation engine.
- Building engine.
- Distribution engine.
- Learning engine.

## Founder Role

Responsible for:

- Opportunity selection.
- Customer understanding.
- Strategy.
- Quality.

## AI Supports

- Research.
- Execution.
- Analysis.
- Automation.

## Avoid

- Building without validation.
- Too many projects.
- Speed without learning.

## Founder Academy Standard

The goal is not more ideas; it is more validated opportunities.
