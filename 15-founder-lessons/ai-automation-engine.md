# Founder AI Automation Engine

## Purpose

Create systems that reduce repetitive work and increase founder leverage.

## Layers

### Discovery

- Find bottlenecks.
- Analyze workflows.

### Automation

- Execute processes.
- Reduce repetition.

### Intelligence

- Analyze.
- Recommend.
- Summarize.

### Monitoring

- Measure.
- Improve.

### Human Control

- Review.
- Decide.
- Maintain standards.

## Avoid

- Automating broken systems.
- Removing judgment.
- Complexity without value.

## Founder Academy Standard

Automation should create more time for important decisions and customer value.
