# Founder Resilience System

## Purpose

Create systems that support long-term founder performance.

## Components

### Thinking

- Separate facts from assumptions.
- Learn from outcomes.

### Learning

- Review.
- Adapt.
- Improve.

### Energy

Protect:

- Health.
- Focus.
- Recovery.

### Support

Build:

- Advisors.
- Communities.
- Relationships.

## Avoid

- Panic decisions.
- Ignoring limits.
- Losing perspective.

## Founder Academy Standard

Strong founders build systems that help them continue learning under pressure.

- YC
- Paul Graham
