# Founder Time Management

## Purpose

Use founder attention on the highest-impact activities.

## Priorities

Focus on:

- Customer learning.
- Product decisions.
- Distribution.
- Strategy.

## Avoid

- Busy work.
- Excessive meetings.
- Too many priorities.
- Activity without outcomes.

## Weekly Review

Ask:

- What did we learn?
- What changed?
- What is the next highest-value action?

## Founder Academy Standard

Time should be invested where it creates the most learning and customer value.
