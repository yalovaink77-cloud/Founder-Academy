# Founder AI Solo Founder

## Purpose

Understand how AI increases the capabilities of individual founders.

## AI Supports

- Research.
- Product.
- Marketing.
- Operations.
- Knowledge management.

## Founder Remains Responsible For

- Vision.
- Strategy.
- Customers.
- Quality.
- Decisions.

## Important Systems

- Documentation.
- Automation.
- Knowledge base.
- Repeatable workflows.

## Avoid

- Tool overload.
- Manual repetition.
- Building without validation.

## Founder Academy Standard

AI turns solo founders into higher-leverage operators, not independent replacements for judgment.
