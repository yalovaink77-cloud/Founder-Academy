# Founder AI Autonomous Company

## Purpose

Understand highly automated company models and their requirements.

## AI Supports

- Research.
- Product.
- Operations.
- Customer systems.

## Human Founder Owns

- Vision.
- Strategy.
- Values.
- Responsibility.

## Requirements

- Clear goals.
- Boundaries.
- Monitoring.
- Review systems.

## Avoid

- Unlimited autonomy.
- Automation without understanding.
- Removing accountability.

## Founder Academy Standard

Automation increases capability; humans remain responsible for direction.
