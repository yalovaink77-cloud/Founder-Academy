# Founder Funding

## Purpose

Understand when and why startups should seek capital.

## Funding Principles

Funding should support:

- Learning.
- Product.
- Growth.
- Strategic goals.

## Options

- Bootstrapping.
- Angel investment.
- Venture capital.
- Revenue-based growth.

## Before Raising

Define:

- Why money is needed.
- Required amount.
- Expected milestone.
- Alternative options.

## Avoid

- Raising too early.
- Spending before validation.
- Ignoring dilution.

## Founder Academy Standard

Money accelerates a validated business; it does not create one.
