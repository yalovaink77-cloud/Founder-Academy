# Founder Network

## Purpose

Build relationships that improve learning, execution, and opportunity.

## Network Types

- Customers.
- Founders.
- Experts.
- Investors.
- Communities.

## Principles

- Give value first.
- Build trust.
- Maintain relationships.
- Share knowledge.

## Avoid

- Transactional networking.
- Collecting contacts only.
- Asking before creating value.

## Founder Academy Standard

A strong network compounds over time.

## Research Basis

- YC
- Paul Graham
- Startup Communities
- High Output Management
