# Founder Adaptability

## Purpose

Adjust decisions based on new evidence and changing conditions.

## Principles

Adaptability means:

- Learn quickly.
- Accept evidence.
- Change methods.
- Protect mission.

## Process

Observe

↓

Understand

↓

Decide

↓

Execute

↓

Measure

## Pivot

Change important assumptions while preserving learning.

## Avoid

- Ignoring signals.
- Changing without evidence.
- Confusing persistence with stubbornness.

## Founder Academy Standard

Be persistent about the mission, flexible about the path.
