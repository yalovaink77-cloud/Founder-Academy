# Founder AI Copilot

## Purpose

Use AI as an intelligent assistant for founder workflows.

## Supports

- Research.
- Strategy.
- Product.
- Operations.
- Marketing.

## Requires

- Company context.
- Customer knowledge.
- Decision history.
- Clear priorities.

## Founder Responsibility

Maintain:

- Vision.
- Judgment.
- Values.
- Decisions.

## Avoid

- Generic AI usage.
- Blind trust.
- Disconnected tools.

## Founder Academy Standard

A founder AI copilot extends capability while keeping leadership human.

## Research Basis

- OpenAI Research
- YC
- Knowledge Management Research
- Lean Startup
