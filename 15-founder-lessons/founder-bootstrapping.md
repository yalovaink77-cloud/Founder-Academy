# Founder Bootstrapping

## Purpose

Build a company using internal resources while maintaining control and discipline.

## Advantages

- Ownership.
- Customer focus.
- Financial discipline.
- Strategic freedom.

## Challenges

- Slower growth.
- Limited resources.
- Higher founder workload.

## Strategies

- Start narrow.
- Generate revenue early.
- Reinvest carefully.
- Understand economics.

## Founder Academy Standard

Bootstrapping is a strategic choice focused on sustainable value creation.
