# Bootstrapping vs Venture Capital

## Purpose

Understand which funding strategy matches company goals.

## Bootstrapping

Advantages:

- Ownership.
- Control.
- Customer focus.
- Discipline.

Challenges:

- Slower growth.
- Limited resources.

## Venture Capital

Advantages:

- Faster scaling.
- Capital access.
- Investor network.

Challenges:

- Dilution.
- Growth pressure.
- Investor expectations.

## Decision Factors

Consider:

- Market size.
- Capital requirements.
- Growth speed.
- Founder goals.

## Founder Academy Standard

Choose funding based on strategy, not status.
