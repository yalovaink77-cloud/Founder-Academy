# Founder Personal Brand

## Purpose

Build credibility and trust around founder expertise and mission.

## Components

### Expertise

Share knowledge and experience.

### Authenticity

Share real lessons.

### Value

Help before promoting.

### Consistency

Communicate regularly.

## Benefits

- Trust.
- Distribution.
- Partnerships.
- Recruiting.
- Community.

## Avoid

- Pure promotion.
- Fake authority.
- Attention without value.

## Founder Academy Standard

A founder brand is built through accumulated trust.
