# Founder Automation

## Purpose

Use systems and technology to increase founder leverage.

## Benefits

- Less repetition.
- Faster execution.
- Better consistency.
- More focus.

## Automate

- Operations.
- Reporting.
- Marketing.
- Support.
- Knowledge workflows.

## Avoid

- Automating broken processes.
- Removing important human judgment.
- Creating unnecessary complexity.

## Founder Academy Standard

Automation should increase value creation, not just reduce work.

## Research Basis

- High Output Management
- YC
- Lean Startup
