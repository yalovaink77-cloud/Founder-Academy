# Founder Moat

## Purpose

Create advantages that protect the company over time.

## Moat Types

- Brand.
- Data.
- Network.
- Distribution.
- Technology.
- Community.

## Principles

A strong moat:

- Creates value.
- Compounds.
- Is difficult to copy.
- Strengthens with growth.

## Avoid

- Feature competition.
- Short-term advantages.
- Technology without customer value.

## Founder Academy Standard

Build assets that become stronger as the company grows.
