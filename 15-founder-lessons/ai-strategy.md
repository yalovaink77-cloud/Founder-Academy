# Founder AI Strategy

## Purpose

Use AI as a strategic capability that improves company value.

## Principles

AI should support:

- Customers.
- Operations.
- Decisions.
- Competitive advantage.

## Process

Identify

↓

Experiment

↓

Integrate

↓

Improve

## Avoid

- AI without purpose.
- Trend chasing.
- Blind automation.

## Founder Academy Standard

AI is a capability, not the strategy itself.
