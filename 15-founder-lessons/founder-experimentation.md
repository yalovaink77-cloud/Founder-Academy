# Founder Experimentation

## Purpose

Reduce uncertainty through structured experiments.

## Structure

Hypothesis

↓

Experiment

↓

Measurement

↓

Learning

↓

Decision

## Principles

Good experiments are:

- Small.
- Fast.
- Measurable.
- Focused.

## Prioritize

Choose experiments based on:

- Impact.
- Confidence.
- Effort.

## Avoid

- Opinions without evidence.
- Large risky builds.
- Experiments without decisions.

## Founder Academy Standard

Founders win by learning faster.
