# Founder AI Knowledge Worker

## Purpose

Use AI as an extension of founder knowledge and decision systems.

## Roles

- Research assistant.
- Analysis assistant.
- Documentation assistant.
- Strategic assistant.

## Requires

- Good context.
- Organized knowledge.
- Continuous updates.
- Human judgment.

## Avoid

- Blind trust.
- Unstructured information.
- Decisions without review.

## Founder Academy Standard

AI should increase thinking capacity while keeping strategic responsibility human.

## Research Basis

- OpenAI Research
- Knowledge Management Research
- YC
- Amazon Working Backwards
