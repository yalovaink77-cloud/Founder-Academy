# Founder Communication

## Purpose

Create clarity, trust, and alignment through communication.

## Principles

Good communication requires:

- Clarity.
- Honesty.
- Context.
- Consistency.
- Listening.

## Audiences

### Team

Communicate:

- Vision.
- Priorities.
- Decisions.

### Customers

Communicate:

- Value.
- Solutions.
- Transparency.

### Investors

Communicate:

- Progress.
- Metrics.
- Risks.

### Community

Communicate:

- Knowledge.
- Insights.
- Value.

## Problems

When mistakes happen:

- Acknowledge.
- Explain.
- Fix.
- Learn.

## Founder Academy Standard

Communication is an operating system for trust and alignment.
