# Founder Reputation & Trust

## Purpose

Build credibility that supports long-term company success.

## Trust Is Built Through

- Consistent actions.
- Honest communication.
- Delivering promises.
- Responsible decisions.

## Areas

### Customers

Quality, reliability, honesty.

### Team

Fairness, clarity, respect.

### Investors

Transparency and responsibility.

### Community

Value creation and contribution.

## Recovery

When mistakes happen:

- Admit.
- Fix.
- Learn.
- Improve.

Trust compounds into a long-term competitive advantage.
