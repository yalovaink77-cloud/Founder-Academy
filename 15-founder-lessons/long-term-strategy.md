# Founder Long-Term Strategy

## Purpose

Build a company capable of creating durable value.

## Principles

Focus on:

- Customer value.
- Sustainable advantages.
- Continuous learning.
- Adaptation.
- Compounding.

## Advantages

Build:

- Brand.
- Distribution.
- Network effects.
- Data.
- Technology.
- Trust.

## Founder Evolution

Early:

- Learn.
- Build.
- Validate.

Growth:

- Create systems.
- Build leaders.

Mature:

- Protect culture.
- Continue innovation.

## Founder Academy Standard

Think beyond the next milestone.

Build systems that improve over time.
