# Founder Legacy & Impact

## Purpose

Understand how founders create lasting value beyond company growth.

## Legacy Types

### Product

Solve meaningful problems.

### Company

Build strong systems and culture.

### Knowledge

Share learning and expertise.

### Community

Create opportunities and ecosystems.

## Principles

- Build useful things.
- Protect trust.
- Think long term.
- Create value beyond yourself.

## Founder Academy Standard

A founder's ultimate impact is the value that continues after them.
