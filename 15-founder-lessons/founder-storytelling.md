# Founder Storytelling

## Purpose

Communicate the company journey and mission clearly.

## Structure

Beginning

↓

Problem

↓

Insight

↓

Transformation

↓

Future

## Audiences

### Customers

Show problem and value.

### Team

Create alignment.

### Investors

Explain opportunity and execution.

### Public

Share knowledge and meaning.

## Avoid

- Founder ego.
- Artificial stories.
- Emotion without evidence.

## Founder Academy Standard

A good story connects human problems with meaningful solutions.
