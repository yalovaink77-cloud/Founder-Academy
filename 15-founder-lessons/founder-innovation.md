# Founder Innovation

## Purpose

Create valuable improvements through new solutions and approaches.

## Innovation Types

- Product.
- Process.
- Business model.
- Market.

## Process

Problem

↓

Insight

↓

Experiment

↓

Learning

↓

Improvement

## Principles

- Start with customer problems.
- Test assumptions.
- Measure value.
- Execute consistently.

## Avoid

- Technology without users.
- Novelty without value.
- Ideas without validation.

## Founder Academy Standard

Innovation is valuable only when it improves customer outcomes.
