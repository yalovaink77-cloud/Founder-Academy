# Founder AI Scaling

## Purpose

Use AI to increase company capacity sustainably.

## AI Supports

- Operations.
- Product.
- Customers.
- Knowledge.
- Team output.

## Before Scaling

Ensure:

- Customer value.
- Repeatable processes.
- Clear metrics.
- Quality control.

## Avoid

- Scaling broken systems.
- Automation without understanding.
- Losing customer focus.

## Founder Academy Standard

AI scaling multiplies strong systems.

## Research Basis

- OpenAI Research
- YC
- High Output Management
- Lean Startup
