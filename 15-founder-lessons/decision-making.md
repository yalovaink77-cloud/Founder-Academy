# Founder Decision Making

## Purpose

Improve decision quality under uncertainty.

## Principles

Balance:

- Speed.
- Evidence.
- Risk.
- Impact.

## Decision Process

Problem

↓

Assumptions

↓

Evidence

↓

Options

↓

Decision

↓

Measurement

↓

Learning

## Rules

Move quickly on reversible decisions.

Analyze deeply on irreversible decisions.

## Avoid

- Ego decisions.
- Assumption-based decisions.
- Ignoring evidence.

## Founder Academy Standard

Great founders build decision systems, not just make decisions.
