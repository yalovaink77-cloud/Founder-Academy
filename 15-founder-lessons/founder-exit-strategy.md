# Founder Exit Strategy

## Purpose

Understand future strategic options for founders and companies.

## Exit Options

- Acquisition.
- Merger.
- IPO.
- Independent continuation.

## Preparation

Maintain:

- Financial clarity.
- Product value.
- Operational maturity.
- Strategic flexibility.

## Principles

Do not build only for exit.

Build a valuable company that creates options.

## Founder Academy Standard

A strong company gives founders choices about the future.
