# Founder Responsible Growth

## Purpose

Grow the company while protecting long-term value.

## Principles

Growth should maintain:

- Customer value.
- Quality.
- Trust.
- Sustainability.

## Balance

Consider:

- Speed.
- Quality.
- Economics.
- Team capacity.

## Avoid

- Growth without retention.
- Broken processes.
- Sacrificing trust.

## Founder Academy Standard

Sustainable growth creates lasting value.

## Research Basis

- YC
- High Output Management
- Lean Startup
