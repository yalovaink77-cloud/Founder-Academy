# Founder AI Founder Assistant

## Purpose

Use AI as a daily support system for founder activities.

## Supports

- Planning.
- Research.
- Communication.
- Knowledge.
- Strategy.

## Founder Owns

- Mission.
- Values.
- Strategy.
- Decisions.

## Requires

- Context.
- Clear priorities.
- Human review.

## Avoid

- Generic usage.
- Blind trust.
- Replacing judgment.

## Founder Academy Standard

AI assistants extend founder capability without replacing founder responsibility.

## Research Basis

- OpenAI Research
- YC
- Knowledge Management Research
