Create AI agents that can understand and use founder and company knowledge.

- Research.
- Decisions.
- Processes.

### Retrieval

- Context.

### Reasoning

- Analysis.
- Patterns.
- Implications.

### Action

- Reports.
- Recommendations.
- Assistance.

### Feedback

- Improvements.
- Updates.
- Accuracy.

## AI Supports

- Retrieval.
- Analysis.
- Documentation.
- Decision preparation.

## Founder Responsibility

Own:

- Knowledge.
- Meaning.
- Decisions.

## Avoid

- Agents without trusted sources.
- AI without context.
- Automation replacing judgment.

## Founder Academy Standard

Knowledge agents transform stored information into usable company intelligence.
