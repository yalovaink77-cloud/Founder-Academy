# Founder Resilience

## Purpose

Maintain effective execution through uncertainty and setbacks.

## Principles

- Learn from failure.
- Protect decision quality.
- Adapt quickly.
- Maintain long-term perspective.

## Practices

- Review outcomes.
- Build learning loops.
- Protect energy.
- Seek support.
- Continue improving.

## Founder Academy Standard

Failure is information when converted into better decisions.
