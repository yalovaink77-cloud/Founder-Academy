Improve founder decisions through structured AI assistance.

- Data.
- Customer insights.

### Framing

- Problems.
- Assumptions.
- Constraints.

### Analysis

- Strategy.
- Values.

- Decisions without context.
- No learning records.

AI improves decision preparation; founders own decisions.
