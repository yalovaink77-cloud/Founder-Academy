# Founder AI Leverage

## Purpose

Use AI to increase founder capability and execution speed.

## AI Supports

- Research.
- Content.
- Product development.
- Operations.
- Decision preparation.

## Human Responsibilities

- Vision.
- Judgment.
- Strategy.
- Customer understanding.

## Avoid

- Blind trust.
- Automating unclear processes.
- Replacing thinking with tools.

## Founder Academy Standard

AI multiplies strong founders; it does not replace founder responsibility.

- OpenAI Research
