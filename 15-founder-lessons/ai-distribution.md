# Founder AI Distribution

## Purpose

Use AI to improve customer acquisition and distribution systems.

## AI Supports

- Content.
- Research.
- Personalization.
- Marketing automation.
- Analysis.

## Distribution Assets

Build:

- Audience.
- Trust.
- Brand.
- Relationships.

## Avoid

- Content without purpose.
- Automation without understanding.
- Vanity metrics.

## Founder Academy Standard

AI amplifies distribution; it does not replace customer value.
