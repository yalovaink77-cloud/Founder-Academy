# Founder Equity & Ownership

## Purpose

Manage ownership decisions to support long-term company success.

## Principles

Equity represents:

- Ownership.
- Risk.
- Responsibility.
- Alignment.

## Consider

- Contribution.
- Commitment.
- Future role.
- Long-term goals.

## Important Concepts

### Vesting

Protects founders and company through earned ownership.

### Dilution

Understand how investment affects ownership.

### Employee Equity

Used to attract and retain important talent.

## Avoid

- Unclear agreements.
- No vesting.
- Emotional ownership decisions.

## Founder Academy Standard

Ownership should support the future success of the company.
