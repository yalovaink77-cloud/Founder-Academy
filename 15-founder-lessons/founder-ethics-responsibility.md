# Founder Ethics & Responsibility

## Purpose

Create companies that generate value responsibly.

## Responsibilities

### Customers

- Solve real problems.
- Protect trust.
- Respect privacy.

### Team

- Fairness.
- Communication.
- Safety.

### Investors

- Honesty.
- Transparency.
- Responsibility.

### Product

- Security.
- Ethics.
- Long-term impact.

## Avoid

- Short-term decisions that damage trust.
- Misleading claims.
- Ignoring consequences.

## Founder Academy Standard

Trust is one of the strongest long-term business advantages.
