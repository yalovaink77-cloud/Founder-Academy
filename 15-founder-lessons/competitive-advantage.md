# Competitive Advantage

## Purpose

Build advantages that make a company stronger over time.

## Sources

- Brand.
- Distribution.
- Network effects.
- Data.
- Technology.
- Switching costs.
- Operations.

## Evaluation

A strong advantage:

- Creates value.
- Is difficult to copy.
- Improves over time.
- Supports growth.

## Founder Academy Standard

Build assets that compound.
