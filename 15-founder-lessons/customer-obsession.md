# Founder Customer Obsession

## Purpose

Make customer value the foundation of company decisions.

## Principles

- Listen before building.
- Observe behaviour.
- Solve real problems.
- Improve continuously.

## Understand

- Customer.
- Problem.
- Current alternatives.
- Desired outcome.

## Methods

- Interviews.
- Observation.
- Analytics.
- Feedback.

## Avoid

- Founder assumptions.
- Feature requests without context.
- Ignoring customer problems.

## Founder Academy Standard

Customer understanding is the foundation of every successful product.

## Research Basis

- Amazon Leadership Principles
- YC
- Lean Startup
- Steve Blank
