# Founder Operating Leverage

## Purpose

Create systems that increase impact without equal increases in effort.

## Leverage Types

- Technology.
- Media.
- Knowledge.
- Network.
- People.

## Founder Evolution

Early:

Do the work.

Growth:

Build systems.

Scale:

Build organizations.

## Avoid

- Manual repetition.
- No documentation.
- Activity without leverage.

## Founder Academy Standard

Great founders create assets that continue creating value.
