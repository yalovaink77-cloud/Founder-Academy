# Founder Speed & Execution

## Purpose

Improve the ability to turn decisions into measurable progress.

## Principles

Speed comes from:

- Clear priorities.
- Fast learning.
- Decisive action.
- Simple systems.

## Execution Loop

Build

↓

Measure

↓

Learn

↓

Improve

## Focus On

- Important outcomes.
- Bottlenecks.
- Customer learning.

## Avoid

- Busy work.
- Too many priorities.
- Speed without direction.

## Founder Academy Standard

Move fast, but learn faster.

## Research Basis

- High Output Management
- YC
- Lean Startup
- Paul Graham
