# Founder Mistakes

## Purpose

Improve founder decision-making by understanding common failure patterns.

## Major Mistakes

- Building before understanding.
- Ignoring customers.
- Lack of focus.
- Hiring too early.
- Scaling too early.
- Ignoring distribution.
- Poor financial discipline.
- Measuring activity instead of outcomes.

## Prevention

- Validate assumptions.
- Talk to customers.
- Focus.
- Preserve resources.
- Measure real progress.

## Founder Academy Standard

Founders should optimize learning speed and decision quality.
