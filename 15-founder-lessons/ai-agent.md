# Founder AI Agent

## Purpose

Use AI agents to increase founder execution capacity.

## Agent Roles

- Research.
- Content.
- Product.
- Operations.
- Customer support.

## Requirements

Agents need:

- Clear goals.
- Defined scope.
- Good inputs.
- Human oversight.

## Founder Responsibility

Maintain:

- Strategy.
- Judgment.
- Direction.
- Decisions.

## Avoid

- Unlimited automation.
- Agents without purpose.
- Trusting outputs blindly.

## Founder Academy Standard

AI agents multiply execution; founders maintain responsibility.
