# Founder Hiring

## Purpose

Build a team that increases execution capability.

## Principles

Hire for:

- Ability.
- Ownership.
- Learning speed.
- Alignment.
- Communication.

## Before Product-Market Fit

Prefer:

- Small teams.
- Flexible people.
- High ownership.

Avoid:

- Premature scaling.
- Unnecessary management layers.

## Founder Academy Standard

Every hire should create more value than complexity.
