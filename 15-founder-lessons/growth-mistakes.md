# Startup Growth Mistakes

## Purpose

Avoid common mistakes that prevent sustainable growth.

## Major Mistakes

- Scaling before Product-Market Fit.
- Chasing vanity metrics.
- Ignoring retention.
- Testing too many channels.
- Ignoring unit economics.
- Copying competitors.
- Hiring too early.

## Prevention

- Validate first.
- Measure customer value.
- Improve retention.
- Understand economics.
- Scale carefully.

## Founder Academy Standard

Growth should amplify a healthy business model.
