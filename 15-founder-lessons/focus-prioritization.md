# Founder Focus & Prioritization

## Purpose

Direct founder resources toward the highest-impact actions.

## Principles

Focus requires:

- Clear goals.
- Strong priorities.
- Saying no.
- Protecting attention.

## Evaluate

Consider:

- Impact.
- Urgency.
- Learning value.
- Strategic fit.

## Avoid

- Too many priorities.
- Distractions.
- Activity without outcomes.

## Founder Academy Standard

Focus creates leverage.
