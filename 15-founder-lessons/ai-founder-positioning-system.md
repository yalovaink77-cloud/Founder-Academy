# Founder AI Founder Positioning System

## Purpose

Create a clear market position using AI-supported research.

## Layers

### Market

- Customers.
- Competitors.
- Alternatives.

### Problem

- Pain.
- Needs.
- Outcomes.

### Differentiation

- Advantages.
- Reasons.
- Value.

### Message

- Story.
- Explanation.
- Communication.

### Validation

- Feedback.
- Response.
- Improvement.

## AI Supports

- Research.
- Analysis.
- Messaging.
- Testing.

## Founder Responsibility

Own:

- Position.
- Promise.
- Strategy.

## Avoid

- Generic messaging.
- Feature-first thinking.
- Trying to serve everyone.

## Founder Academy Standard

Positioning turns a product into a meaningful choice.
