# Founder AI Founder Dashboard

## Purpose

Create a decision center for founders.

## Layers

### Company Health

- Revenue.
- Costs.
- Growth.

### Customer

- Acquisition.
- Engagement.
- Feedback.

### Product

- Usage.
- Experiments.
- Issues.

### Operations

- Processes.
- Tasks.
- Efficiency.

### Strategy

- Goals.
- Risks.
- Opportunities.

## AI Supports

- Summaries.
- Analysis.
- Trends.
- Recommendations.

## Founder Responsibility

Own:

- Meaning.
- Priorities.
- Actions.

## Avoid

- Metric overload.
- Dashboards without decisions.
- Numbers without context.

## Founder Academy Standard

A dashboard exists to improve decisions, not to display data.

## Research Basis

- High Output Management
- Lean Analytics
- YC
