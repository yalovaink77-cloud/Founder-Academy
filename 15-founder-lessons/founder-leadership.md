# Founder Leadership

## Purpose

Build the leadership principles required to create a successful company.

## Responsibilities

### Direction

Create vision and priorities.

### Decisions

Use evidence and context.

### Communication

Create clarity.

### Team

Build ownership and standards.

### Execution

Remove obstacles and maintain focus.

## Avoid

- Micromanagement.
- Unclear goals.
- Poor communication.
- Activity without outcomes.

## Founder Academy Standard

Great founders create systems where teams can make good decisions independently.
