# Founder AI Product Development

## Purpose

Use AI to improve product creation while keeping customer value central.

## AI Supports

- Discovery.
- Planning.
- Building.
- Testing.
- Improvement.

## Founder Responsibility

Own:

- Problem understanding.
- Product direction.
- Quality.
- Decisions.

## Avoid

- AI features without value.
- Technology-first thinking.
- Ignoring users.

## Founder Academy Standard

AI increases product development speed; customers define product success.
