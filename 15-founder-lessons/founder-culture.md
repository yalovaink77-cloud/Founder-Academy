# Founder Culture

## Purpose

Define the behaviours and principles that guide company execution.

## Culture Is

- Decisions.
- Behaviours.
- Standards.
- Priorities.

## Components

### Values

What matters.

### Standards

How work is done.

### Decision Principles

How choices are made.

### Learning

How the company improves.

## Avoid

- Empty value statements.
- Ignoring behaviour.
- Rewarding wrong incentives.

## Founder Academy Standard

Culture is built through repeated actions, not written statements.
