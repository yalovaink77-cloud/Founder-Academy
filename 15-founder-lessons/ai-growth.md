# Founder AI Growth

## Purpose

Use AI to improve sustainable growth systems.

## AI Supports

- Customer research.
- Acquisition.
- Activation.
- Retention.
- Experimentation.

## Growth Requires

- Customer value.
- Metrics.
- Learning.
- Improvement.

## Avoid

- AI without strategy.
- Vanity metrics.
- Acquisition without retention.

## Founder Academy Standard

AI accelerates growth when it improves customer outcomes.
