# Founder Story

## Purpose

Explain why the company exists and why customers should trust the mission.

## Structure

Origin

↓

Problem

↓

Insight

↓

Mission

↓

Customer Value

## Principles

- Customer is the hero.
- Founder provides context.
- Story should be authentic.
- Claims require evidence.

## Avoid

- Artificial storytelling.
- Founder ego.
- Unsupported claims.

## Founder Academy Standard

A founder story connects personal motivation with customer value.
