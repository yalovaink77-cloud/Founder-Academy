# Founder AI Company Building

## Purpose

Understand how AI supports the complete startup creation process.

## Stages

1. Discovery.
2. Validation.
3. Product creation.
4. Launch.
5. Improvement.

## Founder Role

Own:

- Vision.
- Strategy.
- Customers.
- Decisions.

## AI Supports

- Research.
- Building.
- Marketing.
- Analysis.
- Automation.

## Avoid

- Building without validation.
- AI without customer value.
- Too many experiments without focus.

## Founder Academy Standard

AI accelerates company building, but founders create the direction.

## Research Basis

- YC
- Lean Startup
- OpenAI Research
