# Growth Framework

## Purpose

Create a repeatable system for sustainable growth.

## Framework

Understand

↓

Measure

↓

Experiment

↓

Learn

↓

Scale

## Growth Areas

- Acquisition
- Activation
- Retention
- Revenue
- Referral

## Rules

Do not scale before:

- Customer value exists.
- Retention works.
- Economics are understood.

## Founder Academy Standard

Growth should compound customer value, not hide product problems.
