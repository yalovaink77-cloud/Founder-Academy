# Growth Experiments

## Purpose

Use controlled experiments to improve growth.

## Experiment Structure

Hypothesis

↓

Experiment

↓

Measurement

↓

Learning

↓

Decision

## Required Elements

Every experiment needs:

- Assumption.
- Metric.
- Success criteria.
- Failure criteria.

## Prioritization

Consider:

- Impact.
- Confidence.
- Effort.

## Avoid

- Random changes.
- Vanity metrics.
- Experiments without decisions.

## Founder Academy Standard

Experiments exist to reduce uncertainty and improve decisions.
