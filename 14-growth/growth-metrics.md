# Growth Metrics

## Purpose

Measure whether growth creates sustainable customer and business value.

## Funnel

Acquisition

↓

Activation

↓

Retention

↓

Revenue

↓

Referral

## Important Metrics

### Acquisition

Customer discovery and acquisition cost.

### Activation

First customer value.

### Retention

Continued customer value.

### Revenue

Economic sustainability.

### Referral

Customer-driven growth.

## North Star Metric

Choose one metric representing customer value creation.

## Founder Academy Standard

Measure value creation, not activity.
