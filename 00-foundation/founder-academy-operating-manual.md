
## Project Lifecycle

Every project follows:

## Stage 1 — Opportunity

Questions:

- What problem exists?
- Who experiences it?
- Why does it matter?

Tools:

- Knowledge Engine
- Validation Engine

## Stage 2 — Validation

Questions:

- Is this problem real?
- Will people care?
- What evidence exists?

Tools:

- Customer research
- Experiments

## Stage 3 — Building

Questions:

- What is the smallest valuable solution?
- What must be built first?

Tools:

- MVP Build System

## Stage 4 — Market Entry

Questions:

- Who needs this?
- How will they discover it?
- Why will they choose it?

Tools:

- GTM Engine
- Marketing System
- Distribution Engine

## Stage 5 — Growth

Questions:

- How do users receive more value?
- How does growth compound?

Tools:

- Growth Engine
- Customer Success System

## Stage 6 — Business

Questions:

- How does the company create sustainable economics?

Tools:

- Revenue Engine
- Financial Intelligence
- Capital Allocation

## Stage 7 — Scale

Questions:

- How does the company grow without losing quality?

Tools:

- Company OS
- Scaling System

## AI Operating Principles

AI is used as:

- Research assistant.
- Analysis partner.
- Documentation system.
- Experiment assistant.
- Knowledge organizer.

AI does not replace:

- Founder judgment.
- Values.
- Responsibility.
- Strategic decisions.

## Founder Decision Rule

Before building:

Validate.

Before scaling:

Measure.

Before investing:

Understand.

Before changing direction:

Learn.

## Project Application

Every project becomes a real-world laboratory.

Examples:

- Lumora:
  Product, healthcare trust, family memory platform.

- PiercingConnect:
  Content, SEO, affiliate revenue system.

- Kuryele:
  Marketplace validation and operational pilot.

## Founder Academy Standard

Every project should create:

- Knowledge.
- Better decisions.
- Real users.
- Real value.
- Real learning.
