# Project Portfolio

Founder Academy is designed to support multiple products throughout their lifecycle.

The Academy itself is product-independent.

Projects are used only as case studies to apply frameworks and validate playbooks.

Current portfolio includes:

* Lumora
* PiercingConnect
* PC Media Engine
* Kuryele
* Barber SaaS
* Hack Me
* Future products

Every framework developed inside Founder Academy must be reusable across all projects.
