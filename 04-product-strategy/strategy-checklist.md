# Product Strategy Checklist

A product strategy is complete only when all questions are answered.

## Problem

* [ ] The problem is clearly defined.
* [ ] The target customer is identified.

## Value

* [ ] The value proposition is documented.
* [ ] The competitive advantage is understood.

## Product

* [ ] The MVP scope is defined.
* [ ] Features outside the MVP are excluded.

## Business

* [ ] Success metrics are defined.
* [ ] Revenue assumptions are documented.

A strategy is complete when every major product decision has a documented reason.
