# Customer Research Checklist

A product moves forward only after customer research is complete.

## Customer

* [ ] Primary customer identified.
* [ ] Customer problem documented.
* [ ] Current alternatives documented.
* [ ] Buying motivation understood.

## Journey

* [ ] Customer journey mapped.
* [ ] Decision points identified.
* [ ] Trust factors identified.

## Segmentation

* [ ] Primary segment selected.
* [ ] Secondary segments documented.
* [ ] Initial target market defined.

Customer research is complete only when decisions are based on evidence rather than assumptions.
