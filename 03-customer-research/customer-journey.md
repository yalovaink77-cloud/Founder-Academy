# Customer Journey

## Purpose

Understand the complete customer experience from problem awareness to long-term retention.

## Journey

1. Awareness
2. Discovery
3. Evaluation
4. Purchase
5. Onboarding
6. First Success
7. Regular Usage
8. Retention
9. Referral

## Evaluate Each Stage

- Customer goal
- Customer actions
- Customer emotions
- Friction
- Opportunities

## Improvement

Optimize the journey continuously using customer research and behavioural data.

## Founder Academy Standard

Customer experience should improve at every stage of the journey.

## Research Basis

- Nielsen Norman Group
- Strategyzer
- Reforge
- Intercom
