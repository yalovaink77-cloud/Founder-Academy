# Go-To-Market Checklist

A product is ready for Go-To-Market when:

* [ ] Target customer is identified.
* [ ] Problem is validated.
* [ ] Positioning is complete.
* [ ] Pricing is defined.
* [ ] Distribution channels are selected.
* [ ] Launch plan is prepared.
* [ ] Analytics are ready.
* [ ] Success metrics are defined.

Go-To-Market begins only when every prerequisite is complete.
