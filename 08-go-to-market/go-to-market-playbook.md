# Go-To-Market Playbook

## Purpose

Create a repeatable process for launching and growing a product.

## Preparation

- Customer defined.
- Problem validated.
- Solution validated.
- Positioning ready.
- Pricing ready.
- Channels selected.

Use customer feedback and data to improve continuously.
