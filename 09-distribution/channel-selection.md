# Channel Selection

## Purpose

Choose the distribution channels that can repeatedly reach valuable customers.

## Evaluate

- Customer fit
- Cost
- Speed
- Scalability
- Sustainability

## Channel Types

- Owned
- Earned
- Paid
- Partner

## Testing

Each channel requires:

- Hypothesis
- Experiment
- Metric
- Decision

## Metrics

Track:

- CAC
- Conversion
- Activation
- Retention
- Revenue

## Founder Academy Standard

Distribution channels should be discovered through experiments, not assumptions.

- Traction
- Bullseye Framework
