# FA-003 — Project Application System

## Status

FA-003 — ACTIVE

Founder Academy v1 is complete. FA-003 is the application layer that uses the Academy on real products without changing the pre-FA-002 architecture.

---

## Purpose

FA-003 answers one operational question:

> Given a real project, what is the earliest unresolved business problem that should be worked on next?

The system prevents a founder from jumping directly to launch, advertising, growth, or scaling before earlier evidence exists.

---

## Architecture Protection Rule

The architecture that predates FA-002 is protected.

FA-003 must extend the system without rewriting that architecture.

If a future task appears to require a change to pre-FA-002 architecture, work must stop before modification and the founder must receive:

- The exact proposed change.
- Why the existing architecture is insufficient.
- Why the change is necessary rather than optional.
- The risk of not changing it.
- The concrete benefit.
- A non-invasive alternative if one exists.
- The exact files or decisions affected.

No such change may be made without explicit founder approval.

---

## Operating Flow

```text
Select Real Project
↓
Project Intake
↓
Lifecycle Audit
↓
Find Earliest Evidence Gap
↓
Choose One Founder Academy Sprint
↓
Execute
↓
Measure
↓
Record Decision and Learning
↓
Audit Again
```

The project does not move to a later lifecycle stage merely because the product is technically advanced.

Business evidence controls progression.

---

## Lifecycle Audit Order

Every project is audited in this order:

1. Opportunity.
2. Problem Validation.
3. Customer Understanding.
4. Solution / Product Value.
5. Positioning.
6. Pricing / Revenue Logic.
7. Beta / First Users.
8. Go-To-Market and Distribution.
9. Launch Readiness.
10. Analytics and Revenue Measurement.
11. Growth.
12. Advertising Readiness.
13. Scaling Readiness.

The first materially unresolved stage becomes the next work area.

---

## Founder Decision Boundary

The AI may audit evidence and recommend the next sprint.

The founder owns:

- Which real project receives priority.
- Strategic direction.
- Risk acceptance.
- Major financial commitments.
- Final go / no-go decisions.

---

## One-Project Rule

By default, Founder Academy applies one execution sprint to one project at a time.

This reduces context switching and prevents multiple projects from creating a false sense of progress.

Portfolio-level review may still occur, but execution focus remains singular unless the founder explicitly changes the rule.
