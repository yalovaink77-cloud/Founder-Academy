# Project Application Protocol

## Purpose

Use Founder Academy on a real project without skipping lifecycle evidence or redesigning the Academy itself.

---

## Step 1 — Select One Project

The founder chooses the current priority project.

AI may compare readiness, urgency, evidence, and likely leverage, but project priority remains a founder decision.

---

## Step 2 — Build an Evidence Snapshot

Retrieve only the minimum sufficient trusted context required to understand the project.

Evidence may include:

- Current product state.
- Existing users or testers.
- Customer research.
- Validation results.
- Positioning decisions.
- Pricing decisions.
- Distribution work.
- Launch history.
- Analytics.
- Revenue evidence.
- Growth evidence.
- Existing project documentation.

Do not treat old chat statements as stronger than current project source-of-truth documents.

---

## Step 3 — Run the Lifecycle Audit

Audit the project from the earliest stage forward.

Each stage receives one of four statuses:

### VERIFIED

Sufficient current evidence exists to move forward.

### PARTIAL

Some evidence exists, but important uncertainty remains.

### MISSING

The required evidence does not exist.

### NOT APPLICABLE

The stage is genuinely irrelevant to the current business model.

A stage must not be marked VERIFIED simply because documents exist.

Evidence must support the claim.

---

## Step 4 — Find the Earliest Material Gap

The next sprint is normally chosen from the earliest stage marked MISSING or materially PARTIAL.

Reason:

> Later-stage optimization cannot repair missing earlier-stage evidence.

Examples:

- Advertising cannot repair unclear positioning.
- Launch cannot repair missing validation.
- Growth cannot repair weak activation or retention.
- Scaling cannot repair unstable economics.

---

## Step 5 — Select One Existing Founder Academy Sprint

Use the smallest relevant execution playbook already defined by Founder Academy.

Do not create a new framework when an existing playbook is sufficient.

New playbooks are created only when real application demonstrates a repeatable missing procedure.

---

## Step 6 — Define the Sprint Contract

Before execution, state:

### Goal

What evidence or outcome must exist at completion?

### Inputs

What trusted information is required?

### Scope

What is included and excluded?

### Actions

What work will be performed?

### Measurement

What will show whether the work succeeded?

### Completion Gate

What must be true before moving forward?

### Founder Decision

What decision, if any, must return to the founder?

---

## Step 7 — Execute With Low Founder Cognitive Load

Default interaction:

```text
One task
↓
AI performs available operational work
↓
AI verifies result
↓
Founder receives only decisions that genuinely require founder judgment
```

The founder should not become manual middleware for avoidable file editing, repetitive research, context transfer, or verification.

---

## Step 8 — Verify

Completion requires evidence.

Depending on the sprint, verification may include:

- Research-source checks.
- Interview evidence.
- User behavior.
- Experiment results.
- Product tests.
- Analytics.
- Revenue data.
- Conversion data.
- Retention data.
- Git or file verification.
- Founder approval where required.

Generated output alone is not evidence of success.

---

## Step 9 — Record the Application

Every completed application cycle should preserve:

- Project.
- Date.
- Lifecycle stage.
- Initial evidence state.
- Sprint executed.
- Results.
- Metrics.
- Decisions.
- Lessons.
- Next unresolved gap.

Project-specific facts remain project-specific unless broader evidence justifies generalizing them into Founder Academy.

---

## Step 10 — Audit Again

After each sprint, rerun the relevant lifecycle portion.

Possible outcomes:

### Advance

The completion gate was satisfied.

### Repeat

More evidence is required at the same stage.

### Revisit Earlier Stage

New evidence invalidated a prior assumption.

### Stop

Evidence no longer supports continued investment.

### Founder Decision Required

Several reasonable paths remain or consequential judgment is required.

---

## Architecture Protection

This protocol may not silently modify pre-FA-002 architecture.

If such a modification appears unavoidable, execution stops before the change and follows the explicit founder-approval rule defined in `README.md` for FA-003.

---

## Core Principle

> Start from the earliest missing evidence, not from the most exciting tactic.
