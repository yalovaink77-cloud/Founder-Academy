# Beta Strategy

## Purpose

Validate product quality and user value with real users before wider release.

## Beta Goals

Measure:

- Product usefulness.
- Usability.
- Reliability.
- Retention.
- Feedback.

## User Selection

Choose users who:

- Have the problem.
- Match the target segment.
- Provide useful feedback.

## Feedback

Collect:

- Bugs.
- Confusion.
- Behaviour.
- Requests.
- Satisfaction.

## Metrics

Track:

- Activation.
- Retention.
- Usage.
- Errors.
- Conversion.

## Founder Academy Standard

Beta users are learning partners, not just testers.

## Research Basis

- Lean Startup
- YC
- Product Management practices
