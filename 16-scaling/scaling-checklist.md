# Scaling Checklist

Scaling is ready when:

* [ ] Product-Market Fit is proven.
* [ ] Revenue is predictable.
* [ ] Customer acquisition is repeatable.
* [ ] Retention is healthy.
* [ ] Core processes are documented.
* [ ] Team responsibilities are defined.
* [ ] Analytics support decision making.
* [ ] Growth can continue without proportional increases in cost.

Scale systems, not chaos.
