# Launch Playbook

Every launch follows the same workflow.

1. Validate the product.
2. Complete beta testing.
3. Finalize branding and positioning.
4. Prepare distribution channels.
5. Configure analytics.
6. Publish the product.
7. Announce the launch.
8. Monitor performance.
9. Collect feedback.
10. Release improvements.

A launch is a process, not an event.
