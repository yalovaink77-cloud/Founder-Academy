# Launch Playbook

## Purpose

Execute a controlled launch while maximizing learning.

## Preparation

- Customer defined.
- Positioning ready.
- Pricing ready.
- Channels selected.
- Analytics installed.
- Support prepared.

## Phases

### Pre-Launch

- Build awareness.
- Prepare users.
- Test messaging.

### Launch

- Acquire users.
- Measure behaviour.
- Collect feedback.

### Post-Launch

- Improve retention.
- Improve conversion.
- Improve product.

## Metrics

Track:

- Acquisition.
- Activation.
- Retention.
- Revenue.
- Feedback.

## Founder Academy Standard

A launch creates evidence for future decisions.
