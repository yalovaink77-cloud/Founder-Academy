# Post Launch

## Purpose

Continue learning and improving after release.

## Priorities

- Monitor users.
- Collect feedback.
- Improve onboarding.
- Increase retention.
- Improve conversion.
- Measure revenue.

## Metrics

Track:

- Acquisition.
- Activation.
- Retention.
- Revenue.
- Referral.

## Avoid

- Adding features without evidence.
- Ignoring churn.
- Scaling before retention.

## Founder Academy Standard

Launch starts the improvement cycle.

- Lean Startup
