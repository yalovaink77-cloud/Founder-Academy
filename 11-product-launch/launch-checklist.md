# Launch Checklist

## Product

- Core problem validated.
- Product works.
- Onboarding ready.
- Support ready.

## Market

- Customer defined.
- Positioning clear.
- Value proposition ready.
- Pricing ready.

## Distribution

- Channels selected.
- Audience identified.
- Tracking installed.

## Measurement

Track:

- Acquisition.
- Activation.
- Retention.
- Conversion.
- Revenue.

## Post Launch

- Collect feedback.
- Analyze data.
- Improve product.
- Decide next actions.

## Founder Academy Standard

Launch begins the learning cycle; it does not end it.

## Research Basis

- Lean Startup
- YC
- Product Hunt
- Crossing the Chasm
