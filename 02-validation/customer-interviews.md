# Customer Interviews

## Purpose

Customer interviews help founders understand customer behaviour before building products.

The goal is learning, not validation through compliments.

## Interview Process

1. Select the target customer.
2. Prepare assumptions.
3. Conduct the interview.
4. Record evidence.
5. Identify patterns.
6. Update assumptions.
7. Decide the next experiment.

## Principles

- Ask about the past.
- Observe behaviour.
- Avoid hypothetical questions.
- Seek evidence.
- Record customer language.

## Good Questions

- Tell me about the last time this happened.
- How do you solve it today?
- What frustrates you?
- What have you already tried?
- How much time or money does it cost?

## Avoid

- Would you buy this?
- Do you like this?
- Is this a good idea?
- Which feature do you want?

## Strong Evidence

- Existing workaround
- Repeated behaviour
- Time investment
- Financial cost
- Purchase history

## Weak Evidence

- Opinions
- Compliments
- Predictions
- Likes
- Feature ideas

## Founder Academy Standard

A successful interview increases understanding of customer behaviour, not confidence in the founder's idea.
