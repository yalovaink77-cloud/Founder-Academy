# Customer Interviews

The goal of customer interviews is to learn, not to sell.

Focus on understanding real behavior instead of collecting opinions.

Questions should explore:

* Current workflow
* Existing solutions
* Pain points
* Frequency of the problem
* Cost of the problem
* Previous attempts to solve it
* Buying behavior

Never ask:

> "Would you use my product?"

Instead ask:

> "How do you solve this problem today?"
