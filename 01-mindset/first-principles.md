# First Principles

## Rule 1

Solve problems, not build features.

## Rule 2

Customers buy outcomes, not products.

## Rule 3

Validation comes before execution.

## Rule 4

Distribution is part of the product.

## Rule 5

Revenue is evidence of value.

## Rule 6

Measure before making decisions.

## Rule 7

Build systems that can be reused.

## Rule 8

Think in decades, execute in small steps.

## Rule 9

Every failure produces knowledge.

## Rule 10

Never optimize something that has not yet been proven.
