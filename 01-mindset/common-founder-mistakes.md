# Common Founder Mistakes

1. Building before validating.

2. Falling in love with the solution instead of the problem.

3. Waiting too long to talk to users.

4. Treating marketing as something that happens after development.

5. Measuring downloads instead of retention.

6. Scaling before achieving product-market fit.

7. Ignoring distribution.

8. Competing on features instead of value.

9. Launching without analytics.

10. Assuming that a great product will sell itself.
